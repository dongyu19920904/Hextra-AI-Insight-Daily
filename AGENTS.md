# AI Daily Delivery Rule

After a frontend or content change, inspect the rendered desktop/mobile page and both themes. For changes to generated daily content or its display, also generate a complete backend dry-run edition and read the full Markdown: TOP 10 relevance and source diversity, cross-section event duplication, factual links, media, AI fun, FAQ and Aivora links, and readability. A test pass, build pass, or preview defect list is not completion. Fix the smallest responsible cause and rerun the affected checks and full preview until the edition passes editorial review.

Do not overwrite a good published article with a weaker draft. Keep production stable while iterating. If a real external blocker prevents the final content check, state exactly what failed and what remains unverified; do not call the result finished. Report the final preview URL and concrete observations.
