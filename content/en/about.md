**Aivora AI Daily is a news digest for Chinese-speaking AI enthusiasts, developers and tool users.** It brings together product updates, open-source projects, research and practical leads, with links for readers to inspect the original material.

## Sources and editing

The digest uses subscribed feeds, project pages and public articles, with automated processing and AI-assisted editing. Media reports, personal experiences and official announcements are different kinds of evidence. Automated checks cannot guarantee accuracy. For capabilities, prices, quotas and policies, consult the relevant official documentation.

When an error is confirmed, we correct the dated article and related entry points, retaining publication and modification dates. Translations should receive the same correction. See [contact and corrections](/en/contact/) to report an issue.
