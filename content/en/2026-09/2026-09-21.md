## **😄 AI Fun Facts**

### White House Spent 85 Days Negotiating AI Regulation with Anthropic

Politico published an in-depth investigation today reconstructing the regulatory battle between the Trump administration and Anthropic over the Mythos model from April to July. The model could discover security vulnerabilities at extreme speed. After Microsoft got access in late March, they privately warned the government: regular people could now launch complex attacks previously executable only by elite hackers. The Treasury Secretary convened **CEOs of the five biggest banks** overnight. The White House held a Situation Room meeting. VP Vance called tech executives. An administration that had waved the "let AI run free" flag suddenly realized AI could also be a weapon aimed at its own economic lifelines. The eventual executive order had a rocky path. [Politico interviewed over a dozen participants to reconstruct those 85 days](https://x.com/dotey/status/2101802939148910974). That framework is already being left behind by newer models.

### Does Cloudflare Quick Tunnels Require Registration?

No. Cloudflare Quick Tunnels can be used directly without registering a Cloudflare account. Developers just visit https://try.cloudflare.com/ and follow the instructions to open a local localhost address as a publicly accessible online link. The connection can be shut down anytime—perfect for temporarily sharing local dev pages or vibe coding results.
