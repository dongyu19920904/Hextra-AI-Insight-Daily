### 2. Muse Can Log Into Any Site with Your Credentials

**Connector barrier drops to zero.** In a tweet, Xiang Yang Qiao Mu demonstrates that [Muse can receive a website URL, username, and password, then log in and operate on your behalf](https://x.com/vista8/status/2103786960636801361), saving the operation as a connector🔌. Already tested logging into Z-Library to download ebooks. Steam uses tokens, DeepSeek uses API keys, Bilibili uses QR codes—the model never sees the raw credentials. This means you can have an agent operate any service with login state.
