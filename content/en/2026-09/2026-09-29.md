## **🔥 Today's Top 10**

### 1. Manus Drops 2.0, First Major Overhaul Since Splitting From Meta

**The underlying framework just got a generation upgrade.** Baoyu relayed in a tweet that Manus's official announcement states the [new framework cuts token consumption by 23.2%](https://x.com/dotey/status/2104614931962232959), with completion time down **28.2%**. Two new specialized environments — video editing and game development — just landed, plus a standalone app called "Cue" that needs an invite code to try. This marks Manus's first major version bump since going independent from Meta this past September ⚙️.

### 6. AI Digital Human's First Ad Pulled Over False Advertising

**An AI digital human's very first ad gig just backfired.** A Telegram channel reports that a contact lens ad — priced at over 200,000 yuan — featuring digital human "Fang Taozi" was [yanked after users reported it for false advertising](https://t.me/aigc1024/25156). The controversy centers on the voiceover claiming "comfortable to wear all day," a genuine first-person experience claim that raises questions when it's coming out of an AI's mouth — critics say that crosses into **false advertising** territory. Contact lenses fall under Class III medical devices, a sensitive category for ads, and this incident could speed up new rules on accountability in this space ⚠️.

### 7. Jike Blogger Publicly Recruiting to Rebuild an IM Kernel With AI

**Someone wants to rebuild WeChat from scratch, AI Native style.** Jike user Zhao Chunxiang posted that the team is [building out a Rust-based IM kernel](https://m.okjike.com/originalPosts/6aba502d141b85b292ca1246) for their product Cromma. The vision: let users create and distribute in-chat mini-apps with natural language, **zero code required**, with the kernel bound to native, web, and Electron via UniFFI, wasm, and napi respectively. This is currently just a recruitment call — the product itself hasn't taken shape yet 👀.

### 8. Open-Source Project Packs A-Share Investment Research Into a Desktop App

**Tired of juggling multiple apps to trade stocks? Someone built an all-in-one tool.** A Telegram channel introduces easy-stock, an open-source project that [bundles market analysis, sentiment tracking, and AI research into a single desktop client](https://t.me/aigc1024/25142). ### 9. Open-Source Dashboard Breaks Down Claude Code and Codex Costs by the Minute

**Running AI coding tools across multiple machines? Now you can see exactly what you're spending, down to the minute.** A Telegram channel introduces agent-console, an open-source project that [reads local session logs and rolls them up into a cost dashboard](https://t.me/aigc1024/25145). It breaks down token usage across **cache read, cache write, and output**, plus alerts for context growth and cache breaks. Teams running **Claude** Code or Codex across multiple machines can use it to keep tabs on spending in one place 📊.
