### 9. Google Plans to Launch TPU-Equipped Satellite Next Week

**AI chips going to space for inference.** Baoyu introduces in a tweet that [Google plans to launch TPU-equipped satellite next week](https://x.com/dotey/status/2103283524962795707), scheduled for **October 1**.
