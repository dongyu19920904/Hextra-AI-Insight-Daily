### [Google's Acquired Producer AI Opens Testing: One-Stop AI MV Creation Platform](https://x.com/Gorden_Sun/status/2032410450630205598)

Tired of struggling with MV production? Google's acquired Producer AI just passed Waitlist testing, seamlessly connecting music generation and video production. Just input lyrics and it generates music with lyrics (supports Chinese), then creates matching MVs with one click. The music quality is slightly behind Suno V5, but it wins on workflow smoothness—from concept to finished product in one go. ### [Wholembed v3: First Multimodal Retrieval Model to Surpass BM25](https://x.com/Gorden_Sun/status/2032402385566634302)

Retrieval models finally broke BM25's reign. Wholembed v3 retrieves text, images, audio, and video simultaneously, achieving top scores on both LIMIT and BrowseComp-Plus benchmarks. Even more impressive: it's the first large model to surpass traditional lexical matching BM25 on semantic retrieval—previously, even 1B-parameter models got crushed by BM25. It uses late interaction to balance accuracy and efficiency, delivering both database-like precision and vector-search-like similarity matching. For Agent applications, this means stronger information retrieval capabilities.

### [The Ultimate Context Compression Solution: Let Your Agent Search Its Own Memory](https://x.com/dotey/status/2032487717146083350)

Running out of context window? Summarization and sliding windows always lose information. Some developers figured it out: compression inherently loses data—the real solution isn't lossless compression, it's giving your Agent a "memory search" capability. Here's how: the framework automatically embeds each query to recall relevant chunks, each chunk carries topic ID and position info, and the Agent can fetch surrounding context or do semantic search itself. Works great in practice—most of the time auto-recall is enough, and when it's not, the Agent searches a few more rounds. This approach was inspired by OpenClaw, which uses RAG on conversation history.
