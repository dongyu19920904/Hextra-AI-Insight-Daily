### 4. [Stepwise Star Audio Model Step-Audio-R1.1 Tops Global Rankings](https://www.aibase.com/zh/news/24639)

### 5. [Vercel Open-Sources json-render: Making AI-Generated UIs Stop Looking Ugly](https://x.com/Gorden_Sun/status/2011843628558402004)

Anyone who's used AI to generate code knows the UIs often look terrible. Vercel's open-source json-render framework takes a different approach: pre-define a component library, and AI can only pick from those components—so generated interfaces at least won't be a complete disaster. ### 9. [China Telecom Releases GVC: Video Compression Rate Down to 0.02%](https://www.aibase.com/zh/news/24639)

This tech is pretty sci-fi. China Telecom's TeleAI released GVC (Generative Video Compression) technology that compresses video to 0.02% of original size—a 1GB video needs only 200KB to transmit and reconstruct at the receiving end. Instead of sending pixels, it sends "instructions for drawing this frame," and the receiving end uses AI to regenerate it. Currently targeting extreme network environments like satellite communications and ocean voyages, but if it goes mainstream, video transmission could be completely revolutionized.
