### 2. [Tuna-2: Unified Multimodal Understanding and Generation with Raw Pixel Embeddings](https://x.com/Gorden_Sun/status/2049503057571135520)

Tuna-2 goes straight from raw pixels, putting both understanding and generation on the same Transformer, ditching VAE and encoders entirely. Sounds academic, but the payoff is straightforward: AI might soon use the same brain to view images, generate images, and chat about images. If this path works out, multimodal systems get cleaner and skip a lot of middleman hassle.
