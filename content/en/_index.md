### 3. [Tencent Open-Sources Translation-Specific Model Covering 1.8B to 30B Parameters](https://x.com/Gorden_Sun/status/2057479611811053836)

General-purpose large models have always had a translation weakness: the smaller the parameters, the worse the output. Tencent just open-sourced an entire translation-specific model suite spanning 1.8B to 30B, called HY-MT2.

The key stat: small-scale models now match the translation quality of large-parameter general LLMs. What does that mean? Local deployment, cheap inference, translation running on edge devices—all suddenly viable.

For developers building multilingual products, this model suite is worth serious testing—no more forced API calls to expensive large models just to get decent translations. Models are already on HuggingFace, ready to use.
