### 2. Muse Logs Into Any Site Using Your Credentials

**Connector barriers hit rock bottom.** Xiangyang Qiaomu demonstrated in a tweet that [Muse accepts a site URL, username, and password, then logs in for you](https://x.com/vista8/status/2103786960636801361) and saves the operation as a connector🔌.
