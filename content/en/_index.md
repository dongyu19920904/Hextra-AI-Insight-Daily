### 1. [Apple Releases M5 Series Chips: Major AI Performance Boost, MacBook Pro Battery Life Exceeds 24 Hours](https://www.aibase.com/zh/news/25962)

Picture this: your laptop runs local large models 4x faster than the last generation. The M5 Max chip just did it. ### 2. [OpenAI Codex App Officially Lands on Windows, Includes Open-Source Agent Sandbox](https://x.com/op7418/status/2029365178685636899)

Windows devs, your wait is over. Codex App doesn't just support Windows natively—it works with WSL, PowerShell, Git Bash, basically every terminal you can think of. But here's the real kicker: the sandbox mechanism. OS-level restrictions keep AI confined to directories you specify, external network requests blocked by default. AI coding assistants having too much power has always been a hidden risk in the industry. OpenAI just drew a clear line between "empowerment" and "control." And they open-sourced the sandbox, so teams building similar products can reference it directly.

### 5. [TuriX-CUA: Let AI Operate Your Mac and Windows Desktop Like a Human](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzUxNjg4NDEzNA==&mid=2247531901&idx=1&sn=2bdc913f95cfa0523f715a29f8cdd687)

Old AI Agents were stuck in browsers. TuriX takes over your entire desktop. Book flights, check prices, make PowerPoint decks, reply on Discord—anywhere your mouse can click, it can operate. ### 6. [Claude Code Gray-Release Voice Mode: Hold Space, Talk to AI, Write Code](https://x.com/op7418/status/2029388420628529268)

Tired of typing commands in the terminal? Now you can use your voice. Claude Code's gray-releasing voice mode—type `/voice` to activate, hold space to talk, AI executes. Chinese support isn't here yet, but this direction is spot-on. Imagine watching code while saying "change this function's return value to an array," and AI just does it. Programming interaction is being redefined. When Chinese support drops, productivity doubles again.

### 7. [Google NotebookLM Launches "Cinematic Video Overview" Feature](https://www.aibase.com/zh/news/25962)

Dump a pile of papers or notes into it, NotebookLM generates a movie-style explainer video. Not PowerPoint narration—actual cinematic storytelling with visual design and immersive pacing. It orchestrates multiple AI models, auto-deciding visual style, shot rhythm, and narrative flow. Currently only for Google AI Ultra paid users, English only. But once this rolls out, learning changes forever—studying becomes watching movies instead of reading notes.

![Image](https://upload.chinaz.com/2026/0305/6390831666761144033983401.png)

### 8. [Unitree Open-Sources OmniXtreme Humanoid Robot Architecture, Backflip Success Rate Skyrockets](https://www.aibase.com/zh/news/25962)

Robot backflips? Sounds like sci-fi, but Unitree proved it's an engineering problem with open-source code. OmniXtreme uses dual-stage training: first "flow matching" pretraining to avoid traditional RL gradient interference, then executor-aware post-training so the robot learns its motor limits and self-corrects. Translation: it's not blind trial-and-error anymore—it "knows what its body can do." Backflip success rate and inference efficiency both jumped, and it's all open-source. Robotics community has new toys.

![Image](https://upload.chinaz.com/2026/0305/6390830697334464624831191.png)
