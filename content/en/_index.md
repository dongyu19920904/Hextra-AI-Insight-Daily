### 1. Gemini 4 Argon from Google Expands Single Output to 1 Million Tokens

**Output limits just got raised.** [Gemini 4 Argon from Google expands single output to 1 million tokens](https://www.36kr.com/p/4006503753830529), boosting the single-output token cap from 64,000 to **1 million**. This means the model can sustain reasoning across one task trajectory and generate results approaching a million tokens 📄. The model targets enterprise knowledge work in software engineering, legal, and finance, plus cybersecurity defense. Initial pricing sits at **$2** per million input tokens and $10 for output, with cached input priced **95%** lower than standard input. The model will first roll out to trusted cybersecurity defenders through the Fairwind Program, then open in phases.
