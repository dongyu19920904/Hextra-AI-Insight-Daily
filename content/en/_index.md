### 1. [ruflo: Claude Agent Orchestration Platform, Grabbed 2,594 Stars Today](https://github.com/ruvnet/ruflo)

Ever thought about snapping together a bunch of AI agents like LEGO blocks, letting them divide work, collaborate, and learn on their own? That's what ruflo does. Built on Claude, it supports multi-agent cluster deployment, autonomous workflow coordination, and comes with built-in RAG and native Claude Code integration—in other words, you don't have to manually wire up every AI tool anymore; ruflo builds the entire "command center" for you. 2,594 stars in a single day proves developers have been waiting for exactly this. Enterprise-grade architecture plus self-learning clusters—this isn't a toy, it's serious production-level Agent infrastructure.

### 2. [TradingAgents: Multi-Agent LLM Financial Trading Framework, Total Stars Exceed 66K](https://github.com/TauricResearch/TradingAgents)

Picture this: an AI team where one agent watches the market, another analyzes fundamentals, and another handles risk management—TradingAgents brings this division of labor into code. Multiple LLM agents each do their job, collaborating to make trading decisions. Today it gained 2,181 stars, pushing total stars to 66,359. Finance AI has always been a high-barrier field, but this framework turns complex multi-agent collaboration into an out-of-the-box Python library. If you're into quantitative trading, take a serious look—this path is being worn down by more and more people.
