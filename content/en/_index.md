## **🔥 Top 10 Headlines**

### 1. [Claude Plans Mars Rover's Route for the First Time—AI Takes the Wheel on Another Planet](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2017313346375004487)

Used to be, Mars rover engineers had to calculate every frame and verify every step from Earth. Not anymore. Anthropic's Claude just took over path planning for the Perseverance rover—on December 8th, the rover safely drove along an AI-planned route, marking humanity's first time having AI "drive" on another planet's surface. This isn't a lab demo; it's real Mars terrain. The partnership between NASA and Anthropic has taken large language models from chat windows straight into interplanetary exploration. Next up: will AI help us build houses on the Moon?

### 2. [Google Releases World Model Genie 3—AI Game Creation Just Hit Zero Barrier to Entry](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzA3MzI4MjgzMw==&mid=2651014652&idx=2&sn=7631c2bda5d4467489171d12b5e27a26)

Picture this: you type one sentence—say, "a French woman climbing in a world that defies logic"—and AI instantly generates a playable, interactive game world. Google just opened up Project Genie, powered by the world model Genie 3. Describe a scene in text, and AI generates visuals, physics, even character animations—all in real-time and fully interactive. Currently available only to Google AI Ultra users 18+ in the US, but people are already using it to generate Doom-style games. Game developers might want to start sweating.

![Genie 3 Demo](https://linux.do/uploads/default/optimized/4X/3/b/a/3babb067615c96a767c401aedadea813ac5d7626_2_690x430.jpeg)

### 3. [OpenClaw Name Drama: The 100K-Star Lobster Finally Gets Its Official Name](https://linux.do/t/topic/1546498)

This project changed names three times in one week: Clawd → Moltbot → OpenClaw. Why? The original name was too close to Claude, and they didn't want a cease-and-desist from Anthropic's legal team. Now it's OpenClaw, emphasizing "open-source" and "community-driven." It's a locally-run AI assistant gateway that connects Claude, Gemini, OpenAI, and other large models, plus integrates with WhatsApp, Telegram, iMessage, and other chat platforms. You can have it manage your emails, book flights, even automate browser tasks. GitHub stars have already crossed 100K, and a Chinese localized version is live too.

![OpenClaw Interface](https://linux.do/uploads/default/optimized/4X/8/5/d/85d83135aaec2d92f3d6d29df6d48d25668bc28b_2_690x345.png)

### 4. [Alibaba Launches Desktop-Level AI Agent: QoderWork—Control Your Apps with One Command](https://linux.do/t/topic/1545916)

"Organize all the financial reports on my desktop into one Excel file, sorted by date."—You used to say that to interns. Now you can say it to AI. Alibaba Cloud's QoderWork is a desktop-level intelligent agent that can directly invoke Office, browsers, file managers, and other apps on your computer. It's not a web-based chatbot; it's AI that actually gets things done. Currently in closed beta, regular users don't need to configure APIs—just install and go. This might be the turning point where AI moves from "chatting" to "working."

![QoderWork Demo](https://linux.do/uploads/default/optimized/4X/d/a/5/da5d5e6509678de9ed7599d34c0aa7f6a6b5233b_2_690x465.jpeg)

### 5. [Unitree Open-Sources UnifoLM-VLA-0: Giving Humanoid Robots "Physical Common Sense"](https://www.aibase.com/zh/news/25123)

Robots can read images and follow instructions, but do they really understand that "pushing a cup makes it fall"? Unitree's newly open-sourced UnifoLM-VLA-0 large model tackles exactly this. By pre-training on robot manipulation data, the model learns the interaction rules of the physical world. For example, "place the cup at the table's edge"—AI doesn't just know how to place it; it knows placing it too close to the edge will make it drop. This is embodied AI's crucial leap from "image-text understanding" to "physical reasoning," and all model weights are now open-source.

### 6. [AI Studio's Gemini 3 Pro Free Tier Now Capped at 10 Calls Per Day](https://linux.do/t/topic/1546450)

Free-tier users are crying. Google AI Studio's Gemini 3 Pro free calls went from unlimited to 10 per day—exceed that and you get "You've reached your rate limit." Even AI Pro subscribers hit the cap unless they use a paid API key. Though AI Pro's monthly $10 credit can cover it. Some suspect Genie 3's fresh launch is straining compute capacity. Fingers crossed this is just temporary; otherwise, the days of free-tier farming are numbered.

### 7. [Ant Group Releases LingBot-VLA: Dual-Arm Robot Control Enters the Large Model Era](https://www.aibase.com/zh/news/25123)

Training a robot to do a new task used to require thousands of demonstration examples. Ant Group's LingBot-VLA needs just 80. The model uses a "hybrid Transformer" architecture to handle multi-view images and natural language instructions simultaneously, plus incorporates 3D spatial awareness for precision tasks like stacking and insertion. Better yet, it adapts quickly to different robot morphologies, and the full training toolkit and model weights are open-source. Dual-arm robots finally have a universal "brain."

### 8. [Tencent's "Yuanbao Pie" Beta Leak: WeChat and QQ Friends Finally Connected](https://www.aibase.com/zh/news/25123)

Screenshots of Tencent's AI assistant "Yuanbao's" social feature "Yuanbao Pie" leaked from beta testing. The headline feature: WeChat and QQ friends can now listen to music, watch videos, and interact in real-time across platforms. This is Tencent's first time breaking down the wall between its two social ecosystems. AI in group chats acts as a "social butterfly," providing smart summaries, task reminders, and meme generation to keep things lively. Word is Ma Huateng plans to invest 1 billion yuan in red packet incentives to promote it—betting on recreating social magic during Chinese New Year.

### 9. [Anthropic Sued by Music Publishers for $3 Billion Over Copyright Infringement](https://www.aibase.com/zh/news/25123)

Music publishers are suing Anthropic for allegedly mass-pirating over 20,000 copyrighted songs during Claude's training. ### 10. [SenseTime Open-Sources SenseNova-MARS: Agentic VLM Surpasses GPT-5.2](https://www.aibase.com/zh/news/25123)

SenseTime open-sourced SenseNova-MARS, the first Agentic VLM supporting dynamic visual reasoning and deep image-text search fusion. It outperforms GPT-5.2 on benchmarks like MMSearch and HR-MMSearch. This model doesn't just "describe images"—it autonomously plans tasks, calls search tools, and handles complex multi-step problems. Through two-stage training, AI finally has the ability to "think and act independently."
