### 5. Training with reward constraints shows big wins in win rate

**Preference alone isn't enough.** Arena.ai analysis shows that [relying only on human preference training causes reward hacking](https://www.bestblogs.dev/en/status/2106037792870649995) 🎯. Composite reward mechanisms include fidelity-based rewards, assessed through millions of human votes. This approach lifted FLUX.2 by 69 points and Ideogram 4 by 20 points on the leaderboard. Gemini 3.5 as judge shows that adding constraint-based rewards significantly boosts model win rates. Teams training generative models can reference this composite reward design.

### 9. Tavus Griffin video Turing test achieves 48% pass rate
