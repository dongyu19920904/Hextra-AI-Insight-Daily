### 4. [Huawei's "Tao's Law": Chip competition isn't just about nanometers anymore](https://juejin.cn/post/7644060700978970662)

May 25, ISCAS 2026. Huawei's board chair He Tingbo dropped "Tao's Law"—shift the main metric for chip progress from "how many transistors fit in space" to "how much latency can we cut from the system." Simple version: stop competing on process node, compete on system speed.
