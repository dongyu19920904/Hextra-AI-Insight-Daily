### 1. Higgsfield Achieves 80% Gross Margin Through Enterprise Customers

**B2B revenue exceeds half.** Higgsfield's founder disclosed in an interview that [B2B business customer revenue slightly exceeds 50%](https://m.okjike.com/originalPosts/6abfa0fdcfb5d08b3e21a1d2), with OPC revenue accounting for **40%** from customers with high-frequency commercial monetization needs. First-month churn rate is around 30%, but by month 12, enterprise customer NRR exceeds **300%**🔄; one cross-border e-commerce customer upgraded from $99/month to **$6 million** annual payment. ### 7. Two Open-Source Projects Teach AI Agents to Follow Procedures

**Over 500K stars for workflow docs.** [AI Exploration Guide channel compilation](https://t.me/aigc1024/25331), two viral GitHub projects obra/superpowers and mattpocock/skills exceed **500,000 Stars** combined. Projects have almost no code, all Markdown docs📄, purpose is teaching AI how to work. Superpowers' core idea: don't let AI dive into work immediately upon receiving requirements, instead understand requirements first, design solution, decompose tasks, execute, test, review. mattpocock/skills is more direct, author is a TypeScript expert sharing his actual Agent SOP workflow. Perfect for developers configuring workflows for agents like Claude Code, Codex.
