### 8. [SANA-WM: 2.6B Open-Source World Model, Native One-Minute Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15178)

SANA-WM's breakthrough is hybrid linear attention—combines frame-level processing with temporal modeling, outperforming industrial-scale models like LingBot-World and HY-WorldPlay on efficiency while matching visual quality. Open-source, efficient, long-form video—these three together make this the paper researchers and developers should download and run today.
