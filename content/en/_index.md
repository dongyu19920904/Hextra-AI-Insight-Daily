## **🔥 Today's Top 10 Highlights**

### 1. Google Launches Gemini 4 Argon, Expanding Single Output to 1 Million Tokens

**Output ceiling just went up.** [Google launches Gemini 4 Argon, expanding single output to 1 million tokens](https://www.36kr.com/p/4006503753830529). Gemini 4 Argon raises the single-output token limit from 64,000 to **1 million**. This means the model can maintain continuous reasoning within a single task trajectory and generate results approaching a million tokens📄. The model targets software engineering, legal and financial enterprise knowledge work, and cybersecurity defense. Initial pricing is **$2** per million input tokens and $10 per output, with cached input prices **95% lower** than standard input. The model will first be available through the Fairwind Program to trusted cybersecurity defenders, then rolled out in phases.

### 2. Context Optimization Tool context-mode Compresses Sandbox Output by 98%

**Context window pressure just eased.** GitHub daily trending project [context optimization tool context-mode compresses sandbox output by 98%](https://github.com/mksglu/context-mode), reducing occupied space by **98%** through sandboxed tool output🗜️.
