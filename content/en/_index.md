### 2. [OpenAI Codex Launches Chrome Extension: Browsers Are Now Agent Territory](https://x.com/dotey/status/2052492340892221800)

Codex used to live in terminals and IDEs. Now it's moving into your browser. Mac and Windows users can install the Chrome plugin in the Codex app starting today, letting it run multi-tab tasks in the background without touching your active window. Here's the real kicker: it handles work that requires login—checking internal dashboards, refreshing CRM records, completing multi-step forms that need authentication. Codex's playbook is "use plugins when possible, go through Chrome when login's required, mix both." This is the first time Agents have truly reached into authenticated private systems. EU and UK access is pending, but the direction is crystal clear.

### 8. [Mirage: Plug S3, Google Drive, Slack, Gmail into One Virtual Filesystem](https://x.com/dotey/status/2052439472533295553)

Six weeks, rewrote bash, wrote 1.1 million lines of code. Mirage is a unified virtual filesystem for AI Agents. ### 10. [Input Method "Traffic Hijacking" AI Product, Sued for 100 Million Yuan](https://m.okjike.com/originalPosts/69fc598baf7695b4cf5dbdc7)

This one's spicy. Last month, a Hangzhou AI company sued a Shenzhen AI company for 100 million yuan.
