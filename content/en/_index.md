### 10. [RxEval: First Prescription-Grade LLM Drug Recommendation Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14543)

Existing medical AI evals are too coarse—just "what drug for this disease," ignoring dosage, administration route, condition changes. RxEval granularizes to specific drugs, dosages, and routes per prescription, simulating real clinical decision-making.

This benchmark's value isn't "another paper"—it's drawing the line for LLMs entering clinical practice. Without this kind of eval, AI-assisted prescribing stays at "looks good" forever, never actually enters hospitals. For people tracking medical AI deployment, this is critical infrastructure.
