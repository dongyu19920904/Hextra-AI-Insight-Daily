## **🔥 Today's Top 10**

### 1. Manus Launches 2.0, First Major Update After Leaving Meta

**The underlying framework got a complete overhaul.** Baoyu's retweet of the official announcement notes that [the new framework cuts token consumption by 23.2%](https://x.com/dotey/status/2104614931962232959) and completion time by **28.2%**. Two new specialized environments—video editing and game development—plus an invite-only standalone app called "Cue" are now live. This marks Manus's first major version upgrade since going independent from Meta last September⚙️.

### 6. AI Avatar's First Ad Pulled for False Advertising Upon Broadcast

**An AI avatar's debut ad crashed and burned.** A Telegram channel reports that the contact-lens ad featuring digital avatar "Fang Taozi"—priced at over ¥200K—[was yanked after netizen complaints](https://t.me/aigc1024/25156). The issue: the voiceover claimed "comfortable all day," a real-experience statement that raised **false-advertising** flags when delivered by an AI avatar. Contact lenses are Class III medical devices under strict ad regulations. This incident may accelerate clarity on liability rules⚠️.

### 7. Jike Blogger Publicly Recruits Team to Rewrite an IM Kernel with AI

**Someone wants to rebuild WeChat the AI-native way.** Jike user Zhao Chunxiang posted that their team is [assembling a crew for Cromma to build a Rust-based IM kernel](https://m.okjike.com/originalPosts/6aba502d141b85b292ca1246). The vision: users create and distribute in-group mini-apps with natural language—**zero code**. The kernel binds to native, web, and Electron via UniFFI, wasm, and napi respectively. This is currently a recruitment call; the product isn't live yet👀.

### 9. Open-Source Dashboard Breaks Down Claude Code and Codex Costs by the Minute

**Running AI coding tools across multiple machines? Now you can see exactly what you're spending.** A Telegram channel highlights the open-source project agent-console, which [reads session logs locally and aggregates them into a billing dashboard](https://t.me/aigc1024/25145). Teams running **Claude Code** or Codex across multiple machines can use it for unified cost tracking📊.
