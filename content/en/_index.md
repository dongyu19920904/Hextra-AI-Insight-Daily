### 10. [ECHO: Elastic Speculative Decoding Acceleration for High-Concurrency Scenarios](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09603)

LLM inference slowness hurts everyone. Speculative decoding is the mainstream acceleration approach, but crashes under high concurrency—static trees waste compute, dynamic trees mispredict. ECHO proposes an elastic solution using sparse gating to dynamically adjust decoding strategy, maintaining acceleration without wasting verification compute under pressure. Real engineering value for teams deploying LLMs in production.
