### 2. Chinese Gas Turbines Positioned as Critical AI Power Solution

**AI data center power shortage closing in.** According to a 36Kr report, [Chinese gas turbines are viewed as key to AI power supply](https://www.36kr.com/p/3991542332947456). Musk predicted at the G20 that next year will bring significant global power shortages, driven by AI chip capacity growing **40%-50%** annually while available power supply outside China increases only **10%-20%**. Gas turbines offer rapid deployment, emerging as a backup option to ease compute center power bottlenecks.

### 3. Hands-On Comparison: Doubao, WorkBuddy, and Qianwen Office

**Doubao leads in versatility, WorkBuddy wins on stability.** 36Kr ran real-world tests across marketing, content operations, product, and development roles, producing a [hands-on comparison of Doubao, WorkBuddy, and Qianwen Office](https://www.36kr.com/p/3989030731700105). ### 4. Developer Self-Hosts sing-box Subscription Service with Codex

### 8. Developer Implements Semantic Search for Local Photo Library

**No more filename hunting—type "sunset beach" and pull up the photo.** Juejin author One Cat Bo published a tutorial: [after integrating the Lanyun Metagen model, you can search local photos with natural language](https://juejin.cn/post/7687331138987196425). Layered architecture: directory traversal, hash deduplication, and compression happen locally; scene descriptions and tag generation go to the **vision model**; query breakdown is handled by the text model. Full code and Lanyun API integration method open-sourced.
