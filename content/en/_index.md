### 1. [Google Project Genie Opens for Testing: Create Explorable Virtual Worlds with Text](https://x.com/op7418/status/2016925260017451409)

### 2. [Anthropic's Experiment Reveals: Coders Using AI Score 17% Worse on Tests](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2016960384281072010)

Anthropic ran a brutal experiment: one group of junior engineers coded with AI assistance, another without. Result? The AI group finished two minutes faster but scored 17% lower on follow-up tests—basically dropping two letter grades. The culprit: most people treat AI as a "code-writing machine" instead of a "learning assistant." Here's the twist though: the top performers had a secret—they used AI to ask conceptual questions and understand code logic, not to get instant answers. This experiment is a wake-up call for everyone using AI to learn: use the tool right and it's a turbocharger; use it wrong and it's just a tax on your intelligence.

### 3. [Alibaba Open-Sources Qwen3-ASR: 52 Languages + 22 Chinese Dialects, New Speech Recognition Standard](https://linux.do/t/topic/1541516)

Getting AI to understand Sichuan dialect, Cantonese, or Hokkien used to be pure luck. Now Alibaba's cracked the code. The Qwen3-ASR series launched today with the 1.7B version supporting 30 languages and 22 Chinese dialects—even songs with background music get transcribed. The efficiency is insane: the 0.6B lightweight version has a real-time factor below 0.01 per concurrent session, processing 2 minutes of audio per second; at 128 concurrent connections it handles 5 hours of content in 10 seconds. Chinese dialect recognition word error rate is 20% lower than mainstream commercial APIs on average, and English accent tests completely crush GPT-4o Transcribe and Whisper-large-v3. Open-source wins again.
