### 10. [RxEval: First Prescription-Grade LLM Drug Recommendation Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14543)

Existing medical AI evals are too coarse—just "what drug for this disease," ignoring dosage, administration route, condition changes. RxEval fine-grains it to specific drugs, dosages, and routes per prescription, simulating real clinical decision-making.

This benchmark's value isn't "another paper"—it's drawing a standard line for LLMs entering clinical practice. Without eval tools like this, AI-assisted prescribing stays at "looks good" forever, never actually entering hospitals. For people tracking medical AI deployment, this is critical infrastructure.
