### 7. Chrome 155 Will Support JPEG XL Decoding

**Images can shrink another 30%-50%.** **Chrome** team announces [JPEG XL decoding support starting in Chrome 155](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/jpeg-xl-in-chrome). Format supports **lossless compression, HDR, lossless transcode from existing JPEGs** 🖼️, and fine-grained progressive decoding. Implementation uses pure Rust decoder jxl-rs; fuzz testing and AI code review found no memory safety issues. Team recommends trying both AVIF and JPEG XL—not every image beats AVIF. This is browser decoding capability; not all apps and OSes fully support it yet.

### 8. Google Releases Cross-Modal Embedding Model

**Unified space mapping text, code, images, audio, and video.** Google blog shows [EmbeddingGemma 2 totals 740M parameters](https://blog.google/innovation-and-technology/developers-tools/embeddinggemma-2/), activating modules on demand for **on-device semantic search 🔍, cross-modal retrieval, and RAG**. Model supports 8K token context—up to about **5.5 min audio, 29 images, or 58 video frames**. Via Matryoshka representation learning, output vectors can truncate to 512, 256, or 128 dimensions, saving up to 6x for vector stores. Google reports on Pixel 11 Pro quantized config, text-only and full-modal active memory can drop as low as roughly **191 MB and 567 MB** respectively.

### 10. Ted Chiang Explains What AI Writing Lacks

**Number of choices made determines a text's soul.** My Brother Called Tiema relays that [Ted Chiang's 2024 New Yorker piece answers this puzzle](https://m.okjike.com/originalPosts/6ac63d22445b3350d6502205). When you write a 10K-word novel yourself, you're essentially making **10K choices** ✍️. But when you Prompt AI to generate 10K words, you might only make 100 to **1K characters' worth** of choices—AI handles the other 9K. It's this effort of making countless choices that gives writing its soul. Also explains why more precise, effortful prompts yield outputs closer to expectations.
