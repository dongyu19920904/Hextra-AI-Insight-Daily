### 1. [Manus Launches My Computer: AI Agent Controls Your Local PC](https://x.com/op7418/status/2033567417461547144)
Agents used to run only in the cloud. Now they're moving into your computer. Manus's My Computer runs locally, manipulating files and executing automation tasks on both Windows and macOS. Picture this: you say "organize this month's invoices," and it goes through folders, categorizes them, and generates reports on its own. The only question is—do you trust it with your hard drive?

### 2. [Tsinghua Open-Sources OpenMAIC: AI Generates Interactive Courses in One Click](https://x.com/Gorden_Sun/status/2033492767779823828)
Tsinghua went big this time. OpenMAIC automatically generates complete courses: illustrated PPTs, voice narration, post-class quizzes, and even AI classmates arguing with each other during lectures. You ask a question, the AI teacher explains, content syncs on the whiteboard, then other AI students start debating, and the teacher has to explain again—it's so realistic you'd swear you're back in college. You can try it online now, and the effect is so polished it makes you wonder if real people are performing.

### 3. [Alibaba Open-Sources Fun-CineForge: AI Generates Film and TV Voiceovers](https://x.com/Gorden_Sun/status/2033485700717576454)
Adding voiceovers to videos used to require voice actors, recording studios, and post-production editing. Now Alibaba's open-source project handles it all. Fun-CineForge identifies multiple characters, applies voiceover styles on demand, and syncs the generated audio with lip movements in the video. The demo sounds so good you can't tell if it's AI or a real person. For filmmakers, this tool saves a fortune on voiceover costs.

### 4. [Percepta AI Compiles WebAssembly into Transformer Weights](https://x.com/Gorden_Sun/status/2033564595647025177)
This paper is controversial. Percepta AI compiles a WebAssembly interpreter directly into Transformer weights, making the model's forward pass itself a program execution without relying on external tools for math calculations. Sounds cool, but here's the catch: the weights aren't trained—they're compiled directly, more like "writing a program in an unusual way" than actual AI learning. Plus it could be thousands of times slower than native WASM. Is this a breakthrough or just a gimmick? The debate continues.

### 5. [FxEmbed: Fixes Twitter/X Link Embedding Issues on Other Platforms](https://x.com/dotey/status/2033580906699485587)
Twitter links preview poorly in Discord and Telegram? FxEmbed has your back. Usage is ridiculously simple: add "fx" before twitter.com or "fixup" before x.com, and links display properly with videos, GIFs, polls, and multiple images. Built with TypeScript as a Cloudflare Worker, edge computing architecture, free tier handles 100k requests daily. Privacy is solid too—no logs, auto-removes tracking parameters. Open-source, use it if you want.
