### 1. [Manus Launches My Computer: AI Agents Control Your Local PC](https://x.com/op7418/status/2033567417461547144)
Agents used to run only in the cloud. Now they're moving into your computer. Manus's My Computer runs locally, manipulating files and automating tasks on both Windows and macOS. ### 2. [Tsinghua Open-Sources OpenMAIC: AI Generates Interactive Courses in One Click](https://x.com/Gorden_Sun/status/2033492767779823828)
Tsinghua just went big. OpenMAIC auto-generates complete courses: illustrated slides, voice narration, post-class quizzes, and even AI classmates debating in real-time. Ask a question, the AI teacher explains, content syncs on the whiteboard, then other AI students chime in with counterarguments, forcing the teacher to clarify again—it's so authentic you'd swear you're back in a university lecture hall. Try it online now; the output is so polished you'll question whether real people are performing.

### 3. [Alibaba Open-Sources Fun-CineForge: AI Generates Film & TV Voiceovers](https://x.com/Gorden_Sun/status/2033485700717576454)
Video dubbing used to mean hiring voice actors, booking studios, and post-production work. Now Alibaba's open-source project handles it all. Fun-CineForge identifies multiple characters, applies custom voicing styles, and syncs the audio to match lip movements in the video. The demo sounds so natural you can't tell if it's AI or human. For filmmakers, this tool could slash dubbing costs dramatically.

### 4. [Percepta AI Compiles WebAssembly Into Transformer Weights](https://x.com/Gorden_Sun/status/2033564595647025177)
This paper is controversial. Percepta AI embeds a WebAssembly interpreter directly into Transformer weights, making the model's forward pass itself a program execution—no external tools needed for math. Sounds cool, but here's the catch: the weights aren't trained; they're compiled in. It's more like "writing a program in an unconventional way" than actual AI learning. Plus, it could be thousands of times slower than native WASM. Breakthrough or gimmick? The debate's still raging.

### 5. [FxEmbed: Fix Twitter/X Link Embeds Across Platforms](https://x.com/dotey/status/2033580906699485587)
Twitter links preview poorly in Discord or Telegram? FxEmbed's got your back. Usage is dead simple: add "fx" before twitter.com or "fixup" before x.com, and links display properly with videos, GIFs, polls, and galleries. Built with TypeScript on Cloudflare Workers—edge computing, 100k free requests daily. Privacy-first too: no logs, auto-strips tracking params. Open-source, use it freely.
