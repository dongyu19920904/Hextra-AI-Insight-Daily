### 10. [RxEval: First Prescription-Grade LLM Drug Recommendation Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14543)

Existing medical AI evals are too coarse—just "what drug for this condition," ignoring dosage, administration route, disease progression. RxEval zooms in to specific drugs, dosages, and routes per prescription, simulating real clinical decision-making.

This benchmark's value isn't "another paper"—it's drawing the line for LLMs entering clinical practice. Without this kind of eval, AI-assisted prescribing stays at "looks good" forever, never actually entering hospitals. For people tracking medical AI deployment, this is critical infrastructure.
