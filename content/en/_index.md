### 10. [Paper: Random Cloud—Find Minimal Neural Network Architecture Without Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.26830)

Current approach: train big, prune hard—expensive. Random Cloud: skip backprop entirely. As compute gets pricier, "find structure without training" could reshape edge deployment and low-resource scenarios.
