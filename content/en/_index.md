### 5. Reward Training with Constraints Shows Major Win Rate Boost

**Preference alone isn't enough.** Arena.ai analysis shows that [relying solely on human preference training causes reward hacking](https://www.bestblogs.dev/en/status/2106037792870649995)🎯. Composite reward mechanisms include faithfulness-based rewards assessed through millions of human votes. This approach boosted FLUX.2 by 69 points and Ideogram 4 by 20 points on leaderboards. Experiments with Gemini 3.5 as judge show win rates jump significantly when constraint-based rewards are added. Teams training generative models can adapt this composite reward design.
