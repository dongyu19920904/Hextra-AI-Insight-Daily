## **🔥 Top 10 Must-Reads**

### 1. [VS Code Quietly Added Copilot as "Co-Author" to Commits—Developers Are Furious](https://the-decoder.com/microsoft-adds-hidden-copilot-co-author-to-vs-code-commits-sparking-backlash/)

Microsoft silently added a hidden `Co-Authored-By` tag for Copilot-generated commits in VS Code. Looks like a minor tweak, but it hit developers where it hurts: who actually wrote the code, who's responsible, and whether tools should pollute the historical record.

As AI coding becomes mainstream, version control stops being just a technical log and becomes a responsibility ledger. Today's backlash sends a clear message: developers are fine with AI helping, but not with tools rewriting their attribution.

### 2. [Xiaomi Open-Sources MiMo-V2.5-Pro, Targeting Long-Duration Autonomous Coding](https://the-decoder.com/xiaomis-open-weight-mimo-v2-5-pro-takes-aim-at-claude-opus-with-hours-long-autonomous-coding/)

MiMo-V2.5-Pro isn't just another chat model—Xiaomi is putting it into the coding arena with open weights, emphasis on sustained autonomous coding, and direct competition with high-end closed-source models.

What matters is the shift: domestic models are moving beyond general Q&A and pushing into "can actually keep working" engineering capabilities. For developers, the future means more choices for local and private coding assistants.

### 3. [OpenAI Quietly Enabled Marketing Cookies for Free ChatGPT Users by Default](https://the-decoder.com/openai-defaults-to-marketing-cookies-for-free-chatgpt-users/)

ChatGPT isn't just a chat tool anymore—it's an entry point, account system, ad opportunity, and user behavior goldmine. OpenAI defaulting to marketing cookies for free users puts "how does free AI actually make money?" front and center.

For regular users, the real takeaway isn't the cookies themselves—it's the reminder that free AI services have business models too. Going forward, privacy settings on AI tools will look more like browsers and social platforms: you'll need to check them yourself.

### 4. [xAI Lets Grok Users Clone Their Voice—But Added a Verification Gate](https://the-decoder.com/xai-now-lets-you-clone-your-voice-for-grok-with-verification/)

Voice cloning is a killer feature, but it's also a risk minefield. xAI now lets users clone their voice for Grok while requiring verification—a signal that big tech already knows this line can't be crossed without guardrails.

Once this scales, AI voices become identity assets. Being able to prove "this voice was authorized by me" might matter more than sounding identical. That's the real shift.

### 5. [Anthropic Brings Claude for Enterprise to the U.S. Congress](https://llm-stats.com/ai-news)

Anthropic is now providing Claude for Enterprise to U.S. congressional staff and lawmakers. This isn't just selling software—it's a signal that AI companies are entering public-sector workflows.

Government use cases demand compliance, audit trails, and permission boundaries. If Claude can run in a sensitive environment like Congress, enterprises will feel more confident that "AI assistants can handle serious work."

### 6. [Meta and IBM Built an AI Prototype That Can Fix Its Own Code](https://llm-stats.com/ai-news)

Meta and IBM's research pulled "self-improvement" from sci-fi into engineering: let AI watch its own performance, then try adjusting the code. Sounds radical, but the real challenge is verifying each change doesn't push the system off track.

This deserves a Top 10 spot because it points to the next phase of Agents: not just executing tasks, but maintaining and optimizing their own toolchains. Once automation reaches this level, testing, sandboxing, and rollback become core infrastructure.

### 7. [DeepSeek-TUI: Today's Must-Try GitHub AI Project](https://github.com/Hmbown/DeepSeek-TUI)

It might not be today's highest-starred project, but it's more relevant than pure rankings: it works, it runs, and it plugs into real dev workflows. For terminal-first developers, TUI Agents like this will keep multiplying.

### 8. [Nvidia CEO Warns: Don't Oversimplify AI Job Loss Predictions](https://the-decoder.com/nvidia-ceo-warns-against-job-loss-predictions-from-ai/)

"Will AI steal jobs?" isn't fresh news anymore. But Jensen Huang's reminder has real value: don't reduce "roles will change" to "roles will vanish."

### 9. [New Benchmark: Large Models Fail at Simple Ethical Dilemmas](https://the-decoder.com/new-benchmark-reveals-llms-often-fail-simple-ethical-dilemmas/)

Models can write code, reports, and reasoning chains, but put them in ethical gray zones and they stumble. This new benchmark punctures a common myth: sounding like you understand ethics isn't the same as reliably handling value judgments.

This matters for enterprise rollouts. Customer service, healthcare, education, hiring—you can't just test accuracy. You need to test boundaries, refusals, and consistency. The more human-like AI gets, the more you need to validate it like a person, not a tool.
