### 9. [AI Cracks Dia Browser Cookie Encryption, Actually Succeeds](https://x.com/vista8/status/2029971883811287277)

Dia browser uses a pretty complex custom encryption: v10 prefix + 16-byte nonce + AES ciphertext, then after decryption there's another 16-byte header, and the actual cookie value starts at byte 17. Sounds hardcore, right? AI just walked through it step-by-step and **cracked it**. Two takeaways: AI's reverse-engineering chops are wild, and every security team just got a wake-up call—your encryption scheme? AI might understand it better than you do.
