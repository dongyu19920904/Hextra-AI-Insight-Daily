## **🔥 Today's Top 10**

### 1. Manus Drops 2.0, First Major Overhaul Since Leaving Meta

**The underlying framework got a full generation upgrade.** As shared by Baoyu on X, **Manus**'s official announcement states the [new framework cuts token consumption by 23.2%](https://x.com/dotey/status/2104614931962232959), with completion time down **28.2%**. Two new specialized environments landed for video editing and game dev, plus a standalone app called "Cue" that needs an invite code to try. This marks Manus's first major version upgrade since going independent from Meta this past September ⚙️.

### 2. AMD Acquires Fei-Fei Li's World Labs for $8.2 Billion

**A chip company just bought a world model startup.** As shared by Baoyu on X, once the deal closes [Fei-Fei Li will join AMD as Executive VP and Chief Scientist](https://x.com/dotey/status/2104695874328912364). The acquisition price lands around **$8.2 billion**, and **World** Labs' product Marble can turn text or images into explorable 3D scenes. AMD is using this move to go head-to-head with Nvidia in the world model race 🔬.

### 6. AI Digital Human's First Ad Pulled Over False Advertising

**An AI digital human's debut ad campaign just crashed and burned.** According to a Telegram channel post, the [colored contact lens ad featuring digital human "Fang Taozi," which reportedly cost over 200,000 yuan, got pulled fast after being reported by users](https://t.me/aigc1024/25156). The controversy centers on a voiceover line claiming "comfortable all day long" — a real-experience claim voiced by an AI avatar that's now being flagged as **false advertising**. Colored contacts fall under Class III medical devices, a sensitive category for ads, and this incident could speed up new rules on liability in this space ⚠️.

### 7. Jike Blogger Publicly Recruiting to Rebuild an IM Core with AI

**Someone wants to rebuild WeChat from scratch, AI-native style.** Jike user Zhao Chunxiang posted that the team behind their product Cromma is [building a Rust-based IM core](https://m.okjike.com/originalPosts/6aba502d141b85b292ca1246). The vision: users create and share group mini-apps using natural language, **zero code required**, with the core bound to native, Web, and Electron via UniFFI, wasm, and napi respectively. This is currently just a recruitment call — the product itself hasn't taken shape yet 👀.

### 8. Open-Source Project Packs A-Share Stock Research Into One Desktop App

**Tired of juggling multiple apps to trade stocks? Someone built an all-in-one tool.** According to a Telegram channel post, the open-source project easy-stock [bundles market analysis, sentiment tracking, and AI-powered research into a single desktop client](https://t.me/aigc1024/25142). The source is public and runs with **local deployment**, but it's limited to personal, non-commercial use only. Retail investors who like tinkering with tools can give it a spin — just double-check the licensing terms before any commercial use 🖥️.

### 9. Open-Source Dashboard Breaks Down Claude Code and Codex Spending by the Minute

**Running AI coding tools across multiple machines? Now you can actually see where the money's going.** According to a Telegram channel post, the open-source project agent-console [reads local session logs and rolls them up into a single cost dashboard](https://t.me/aigc1024/25145). It breaks down token usage across **cache read, cache write, and output**, plus flags context growth and cache breaks. Teams running **Claude** Code or Codex across multiple machines can use it to keep tabs on spending in one place 📊.

### 10. Manus Podcast Debate: Who's Going to Build the Personal Agent First

**Manus's latest update has everyone rethinking who'll crack the personal agent race first.** Blogger Vista8 commented that [a Manus podcast sparked debate over who'll build the personal agent first](https://x.com/vista8/status/2104596819900879126). His take: if it weren't for the acquisition drama getting in the way, **Personal Agent**-type products should've come out of the **Manus** team first. This is just the blogger's personal opinion — Manus hasn't made any official statement on this 💭.
