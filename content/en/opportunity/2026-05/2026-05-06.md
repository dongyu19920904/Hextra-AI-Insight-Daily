## Today's Top Pick
### You don't need to understand GPT-5.5, but you can help people use it

OpenAI switched ChatGPT's default model to GPT-5.5 Instant today with a full rollout—hundreds of millions of people opened it to the new version. The catch? Most people have no idea how to apply this "more accurate version" to their actual work. They just feel like "it's faster now" and keep asking random questions.

What you can sell today isn't accounts—it's a "pre-tuned prompt package for a specific profession." Think customer service reply templates for e-commerce, follow-up scripts for sales, topic frameworks for short-video creators. Buyers paste it straight into ChatGPT and go—no trial and error. With GPT-5.5 cutting hallucinations, these templates produce better output too. Launch this today and the timing is perfect.

- **Who it's for:** People with ChatGPT accounts who don't know how to use them well, especially e-commerce support reps, salespeople, and short-video creators who write repetitive copy daily
- **Where the money comes from:** GPT-5.5 Instant rolled out today, buyers see the news and wonder "what can I use this for"—a "ready-to-use professional template pack" sells better than any tutorial; the 52.5% hallucination reduction is today's best proof point
- **Easiest way to sell it:** Start with one profession—like "E-commerce Customer Service ChatGPT Prompt Pack, 10 scenarios, buy and use immediately"—price low to drive traffic, then upsell Q&A or advanced packages later
- **What to do first today:** Pick one profession you know best, compile 5-10 prompts, screenshot the input→output results, and you can list it or post to your community tonight
- **Copy you can post today:** "ChatGPT quietly upgraded today and got way more accurate. But most people still don't know how to ask the right questions. I put together an e-commerce customer service prompt pack—10 high-frequency scenarios, buy it and paste it straight in, no guesswork needed."
- **Image suggestion:** Screenshot a ChatGPT conversation using your template to handle a customer service question—show "before (messy questions)" on the left and "after (using the template)" on the right for maximum contrast

## This Week's Options
### Helping people make PPTs with AI is easier to sell than you think

open-slide just launched—an open-source project that lets AI agents generate editable, playable slideshows with animation support. Tech circles are talking about it, but the real opportunity isn't there—it's with regular people who do presentations, proposals, and lectures every week.

You don't need heavy investment right now because the project is still being refined (PPTX export isn't solid yet), but you can test-launch an "AI PPT generation starter pack" and see if anyone bites. If they do, go deeper.

- **Who it's for:** Office workers who make PPTs constantly but hate the hassle, teachers, content creators—and sellers who want to save them time
- **How to test it:** Run open-slide yourself, record a 30-second demo of "input topic → generate slides → edit online," post it with copy like "No Photoshop, no Canva, AI makes your PPT for you," and test the reaction in your circle or community
- **Why not go all-in yet:** The project doesn't support PPTX export yet, and most buyers will eventually need to export files. Heavy investment now means getting stuck when they ask "can I download this?"—test small first, go big once the feature is solid
- **Image suggestion:** Screenshot a nice-looking open-slide preview, pick a visually strong theme, add text overlay like "AI makes PPT in 30 seconds"
