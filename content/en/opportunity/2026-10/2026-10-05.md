- **Minimum delivery:** **"Strata Deployment Package"** includes: ① Complete deployment steps (from download to first inference), ② Before/after VRAM and speed comparison screenshots, ③ Configuration templates for different GPUs, ④ Email support for 3 business days. Explicitly excludes: model fine-tuning, performance optimization, commercial solutions. Buyer can independently reproduce on same-spec hardware.

- **48-hour validation:** ① Deploy Strata from scratch in test environment, document time per step, friction points, final inference speed and VRAM usage; ② Create deployment documentation and comparison screenshots; ③ Find 3-5 developers with GPUs who want to run large models locally, show them samples, ask via DM or community "would you pay for this ready-to-run package, and what might you offer" — record feedback.

First sale fixed to single customer, single hardware config, one deployment plus 3 days support. Later can upgrade to other hardware or monthly ongoing service. Asset building: distill deployment experience into configuration template library, second customer delivery cost drops 40%+.

- **Risks & stop conditions:** Medium after-sales risk. Model updates may break compatibility—must state clearly "based on 2026-10-05 version, subsequent updates validated by customer." Hardware diversity risk medium—different GPUs need different quantization approaches, can't promise consistency across all hardware. Stop conditions: ① Can't reproduce deployment, ② All 5 users say they can figure it out themselves, ③ Official major update breaks existing approach but no stable new version available.
