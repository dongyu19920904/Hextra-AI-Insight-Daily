## Today's Lead Opportunity

### Validate Whether Academic Research Trackers Will Pay for an AI Preprint Structured Interpretation Package

**The official repository is already public and the material is obtainable today, but whether anyone will pay to understand it is an unvalidated hypothesis.** OpenAI itself says these three manuscripts require "understanding, verification, and synthesis." The real opportunity lies in whether people are repeatedly digging through the PDFs yet unable to form quick judgments.

- **Evidence and confidence:** Medium. [OpenAI official release page: confirms three sets of research materials are publicly available](https://openai.com/index/building-scientific-understanding-with-academic-communities/); [openai/math repository: confirms preprints are archived and the repository is active (Apache-2.0, updated 2026-10-28)](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/preprints/The-Exponential-Time-Hypothesis-is-False-October-25-2026/paper.pdf). Gap: the candidate inputs this round provided no target-user interviews or evidence of paying behavior.
- **Target pool and manual approach:** Unvalidated hypothesis: tech-media editors and academic content creators may currently be reading the original text in full and translating it paragraph by paragraph just to produce their pieces; whether they would pay to skip that full-read step is unconfirmed.
- **Minimum deliverable:** An "AI Preprint Structured Interpretation Package" containing: ① a one-sentence summary of the core claim; ② a glossary (5–8 terms with Chinese explanations); ③ a list of existing open questions; ④ a citable judgment summary. Acceptance criterion: the buyer can write a well-sourced commentary without opening the original paper. Does not include mathematical proof verification; does not constitute academic consulting.
- **48-hour validation:** First, produce a complete sample using the ETH preprint and record actual time spent. Send screenshots of the sample to 3–5 tech-media editors or academic content-creator accounts and ask: "If I published one of these interpretations per week, would you buy it, and roughly what's your budget?" Observe whether anyone proactively asks about pricing or requests the full version.
- **First sale and repeat purchases:** Test outreach message: "AI just published three math preprints. I've put together a structured interpretation — want to take a look and see if it's worth your time?" The first sale covers only the ETH paper and delivers the four items above, with no Q&A included. If the first sale closes, a "Weekly AI Research Speed-Read Package" subscription could follow; the glossary and format templates are reusable and can reduce marginal costs, but this depends entirely on whether anyone pays in the first round.
- **Risks and stopping points:** Compliance risk **medium**: the preprints have not been peer-reviewed; deliverables must be labeled "for reference only — source is an unreviewed preprint." Stop conditions: 5 users view the sample and none asks about pricing; buyers indicate "reading the original is good enough for me"; or the cost of producing the sample exceeds the price buyers are willing to accept.
