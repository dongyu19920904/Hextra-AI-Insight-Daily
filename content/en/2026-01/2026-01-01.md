## 🔥 Top 10 Heavy Hitters

### 1. [Zhihui Jun Releases Small-Scale Full-Body Force-Controlled Humanoid Robot Q1](https://x.com/dotey/status/2006422559504609678)

Remember that genius kid who built an 'electronic spine'? Well, Zhihui Jun is at it again! This time, he's unveiled a **small-scale, full-body force-controlled humanoid robot** called Prime Quester1 (Q1). Don't let its size fool you; this little guy's full-body force control means it's not some clunky mechanical arm. Nope, it can interact with its environment as smoothly and flexibly as a human. The official slogan boldly declares, 'Your first personal robot is HERE,' and they're not kidding around. **Finally, a domestic player has officially entered the personal robot race!** 🤖

### 2. [South Korea Open-Sources Three Large Models at Once, Government Backs "Sovereign AI"](https://www.bestblogs.dev/en/status/2006369448551141506)

South Korea is making waves in the open-source AI scene! The Hugging Face CEO even tweeted his amazement at the country's exploding open-source AI ecosystem. This week alone, South Korea dropped three major open-source models: LG's K-Exagone (236B MoE), Upstage's Solar Open (102B), and SK Telecom's A.X K1 (519B, slated for Jan 4 release). All this is happening with the full backing of the Korean government's 'Sovereign AI Foundation Model' project. **While others are still debating whether to open-source, South Korea has already made it a national strategy.** Expect the open-source AI competition to get even fiercer in 2026. 🇰🇷🔥

### 3. [Alibaba Open-Sources Qwen-Image-2512: AI-Generated Images Finally Less "AI-ish"](https://www.bestblogs.dev/en/status/2006294325240668255)

Alibaba's Qwen team just dropped their next-gen image generation model, Qwen-Image-2512. The big news? **Human faces finally look legit!** Remember that uncanny, plasticky vibe from AI-generated portraits? Well, the official word is they've optimized a ton, leading to richer facial details, finer natural textures (think landscapes, hair), and boosted text rendering. After over 10,000 rounds of blind testing on AI Arena, they're claiming it's the strongest open-source image model out there, ready to go head-to-head with closed-source giants. **Wanna give it a spin? You can try it directly on Qwen Chat or Hugging Face.** ✨

### 4. [Andrew Ng's Annual AI Summary: 2025 is the First Year of a New Industrial Era](https://www.bestblogs.dev/en/article/2867a6f1)

Andrew Ng just dropped his annual AI summary, highlighting four key points: **1) Model inference capability is now standard**, no longer a privilege for a select few; **2) AI talent is seeing skyrocketing value**, with Meta leading the charge on 'superstar contracts'; **3) Data centers are the new 'steel mills'** of this era, ushering in an asset-heavy age; and **4) AI Agents are reshaping programming**, with code completion rates commonly exceeding 80%. His advice for developers? Don't just watch from the sidelines – get learning! Courses, practical experience, and papers are all essential. 📚💡

### 5. [Google Gemini Open-Sources "Computer Use" Preview](https://github.com/google-gemini/computer-use-preview)

Google Gemini just quietly dropped a project on GitHub called `computer-use-preview`, already racking up over 2300 stars. While there's no official description, the name itself hints at a Gemini-powered 'computer control' capability preview – much like Anthropic's earlier Claude Computer Use. **AI isn't just for chatting anymore; it's about to start helping you operate your computer!** This space is heating up fast. 💻🔥

### 6. [TrendRadar: AI Helps You Understand Hot Topics Across 35 Platforms](https://github.com/sansan0/TrendRadar)

TrendRadar is here to tackle information overload, a common modern-day ailment. This open-source project monitors **hot topics across 35 platforms** like Douyin, Zhihu, Bilibili, and Wall Street News, using AI to filter, analyze, and push updates to you. It supports various notification channels, including WeChat Work, Feishu, DingTalk, and Telegram, and can even perform deep analysis using natural language (think trend tracking, sentiment analysis, etc.). **Deploy it in 30 seconds, get notified in 1 minute – perfect for media pros and investors who need to stay on top of trends.** With over 40,000 stars, it's clearly a hit for a reason! 📈

### 7. [Chatterbox: Open-Source Real-time Voice Conversation Framework](https://github.com/resemble-ai/chatterbox)

Resemble AI just open-sourced Chatterbox, a real-time voice framework designed to zap that 'laggy' feeling from AI voice interactions. It pipeline-optimizes three key stages: Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR), Large Language Model (LLM) processing, and Text-to-Speech (TTS). The magic? **TTS starts generating the initial audio even before the LLM has finished its full response!** This streaming approach makes conversations sound way more natural. Developers building virtual humans or smart customer service agents should definitely check it out – it's already racked up nearly 20,000 stars. 🗣️✨

### 8. [Vibe-Kanban: A Kanban Tool for the AI Era](https://github.com/BloopAI/vibe-kanban)

Vibe-Kanban is here, bringing the 'Vibe Coding' trend to Kanban tools! This project claims it can boost the efficiency of coding agents like Claude Code and Codex by a whopping 10x. It's not about manually tapping out task cards; instead, it **deeply integrates AI to help you break down vague ideas into actionable tasks.** Lighter than Jira, and it just *gets* developers better than Notion. With 10,000 stars, it's perfect for fast-paced small teams and solo devs. 🚀

### 9. [Nvidia Acquires Israeli AI Startup for $2 Billion](https://www.qbitai.com/2025/12/366314.html)

Nvidia's 'Old Huang' (Jensen Huang) is at it again! Reports say Nvidia is acquiring an Israeli AI startup for **over $2 billion**. The specific company hasn't been officially announced, but Nvidia is clearly a pro at this 'acquire-to-hire' game – why wait for talent to slowly grow when you can just buy the whole darn team? **In today's red-hot AI talent war, having deep pockets means you can pretty much do whatever you want.** 💰

### 10. [2026 AI Predictions: Edge AI, Agent Harness, Human Authentication](https://www.bestblogs.dev/en/status/2006269346448863529)
