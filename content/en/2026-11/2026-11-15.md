### 2. ChatGPT Wallet Lets AI Agents Pay on Your Behalf

**ChatGPT adds AI-agent payment capability 🔒.** OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet on November 13 in partnership with American Express, Mastercard, and Visa. [Lotook has documented the payment flow and security mechanisms](https://lotook.com/articles/chatgpt-wallet/). Before each purchase, the agent displays the item, total price, and the card to be charged, requiring face ID or fingerprint confirmation. Merchants receive a token issued by the payment network and never see the **actual card number**. Currently available only to **US Pro users**.

### 5. Hassabis Warns That Closed-Loop AI Self-Improvement Is Hard to Control

**Hassabis identifies a control blind spot in recursive self-improvement.** The AI Exploration Guide channel summarized the interview in which [Hassabis warned that closed-loop AI self-improvement is hard to control](https://t.me/aigc1024/27038). His concern is **closed-loop RSI** without human review: the system improves faster while becoming harder for humans to understand and intervene in. He also noted that current AI systems do not, in his view, possess **consciousness**, and that intelligence and consciousness can be discussed separately.

### 9. Rust 1.100 Stabilizes the Never Type and Custom Allocators

**Rust extends the `!` type to all nameable type positions.** The [official Rust blog published the 1.100 release notes](https://blog.rust-lang.org/2026/11/12/Rust-1.100.0/) alongside stabilization of the core `Allocator` trait, though collection-related interfaces are not yet fully complete. The new `DropGuard` runs cleanup on panic, and Cargo gains an **unused-dependency check** and an optional minimum **publish-time setting**. Projects on older editions should watch for changes in type-inference fallback behavior.

### 10. Engineer Rewires Coway Air Purifier from Cloud to Local

**The key to bypassing the cloud was hidden in the provisioning data.** Scott documented the reverse-engineering process in detail on his blog, [finding that the Bluetooth provisioning packet contains a certificate download URL](https://scottjg.com/posts/2026-11-11-reverse-engineering-my-coway-air-purifier-to-ditch-the-cloud/). Substituting a **local address** causes the device to connect to a self-hosted server. The final setup uses Web Bluetooth for provisioning and local MQTT to connect to Home Assistant, with **AI assistance used to reconstruct part of the provisioning flow**. A useful reference for anyone who doesn't want to depend on a manufacturer's cloud service.
