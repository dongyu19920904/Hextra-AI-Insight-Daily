### 3. Claude Agent Independently Completes Two Black Hole Theory Calculations

**AI agents enter frontier physics.** 📌 An [Anthropic research note](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-black-hole-physics) shows that a Claude agent worked through **22** candidate problems over roughly six weeks and completed two previously unsettled calculations. The first confirmed a weak-coupling phase transition is discontinuous; the second established an upper bound on the mass of the lightest black hole of approximately **c/9.1266**. The bootstrap result was verified in Lean and reviewed by physicists. Behavior in the intermediate coupling regime remains undetermined, and the results are limited to theoretical models and do not directly describe observable black holes.

### 4. AI Agent Designs Frostbite CPU from Scratch, Achieving 4.59x Energy Efficiency

**AI completes a full chip design pipeline.** 🧪 An [Architect Labs report](https://architectlabs.com/blog/frostbite) describes an agent exploring compiler, architecture, RTL, and physical layout in parallel, with a 7nm design reaching **500 MHz**. Across **25** workloads, the four-cluster compute unit achieves a geometric mean of 4.59x performance-per-watt, with a held-out evaluation set showing 3.83x. These are pre-tapeout figures; the comparison core is unnamed, and out-of-order cores and SPEC benchmarks are still planned. RTL and ISA have not been released.

### 5. Signal Counters System AI Reading Decrypted Chat Screens

**Signal extends its privacy protections to the device level.** An [official blog post](https://signal.org/blog/protecting-your-privacy/) states that iOS 27 already blocks in-app screenshots, and on Android environments where an AI assistant is detected reading the screen, the screen security setting **activates automatically** 📄. Users can disable this manually, but doing so may allow the other party's messages to be captured by the system as well. ### 7. Ruby Case-Conversion Bug Can Trigger RCE Under Default Rails Configuration

**Rails applications need to upgrade Ruby immediately.** 📌 [Veria Labs has disclosed](https://verialabs.com/ruby2shell/) that CVE-2026-132825 stems from certain single-byte encodings producing two characters when ß is uppercased, causing an out-of-bounds write in a loop. Default-configuration **Rails** and classic-style Sinatra are reachable, and the fix is in **Ruby 3.3.14, 3.4.13, and 4.0.10**. Ruby 3.2 and older are end-of-life; bundled runtimes such as GitLab require a vendor package update, and upgrading the host Ruby does not fix a bundled version.
