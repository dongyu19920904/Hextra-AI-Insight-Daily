### 2. Open-Source World Model PAN 2 Surpasses NVIDIA Cosmos

**Open-source diffusion model outperforms commercial benchmarks.** PAN 2, released by MBZUAI, combines a 29B-parameter diffusion video generator with LLM reasoning. A [technical summary compiled by BestBlogs](https://www.bestblogs.dev/en/status/2120214912735854713?utm_source=rss&utm_medium=feed&utm_campaign=resources&entry=rss_article_item) shows a task-planning success rate of **34%**. Model weights are **58 GB** and available for download under the Apache 2.0 license. Developers working on autonomous driving or robotics simulation can pull the weights directly from Hugging Face, though physical consistency remains unstable in roughly two-thirds of simulated scenarios.

### 4. MiniMax Open-Sources H3-Image Generation Model

**MiniMax image model weights are now publicly available.** Gorden Sun posted that [MiniMax has open-sourced H3-Image](https://x.com/Gorden_Sun/status/2120161175724007651), an image generation model derived from the H3 video model. It supports generating images at **4K×4K** resolution and accepts up to **16** reference images. The model page briefly returned a 404 at launch, with weights still uploading. Developers who need high-resolution image generation or multi-reference image compositing can watch the Hugging Face page for the upload to complete.

### 9. AI4M Closed-Door Session: China's Manufacturing Data Volume Does Not Automatically Equal Advantage

**Large data volume does not automatically translate into an AI materials advantage.** Ji Shisan's [notes from the Future Lightcone AI for Materials closed-door session](https://m.okjike.com/originalPosts/6af2acc4320fd7e1b533cb73) gathered perspectives from over 20 entrepreneurs, scientists, and investors. The core conclusion: more factories and longer supply chains, but records of failed samples are scattered and inconsistently formatted, with no way to link formulations to processes. The real barrier for **automated laboratories** lies in stable execution over long workflows — a successful single-step demo does not mean reliable operation at scale. Teams entering the **AI4M** space need to start by planning their data infrastructure, not their model capabilities.

## **◎ Industry Shifts and Personal Impact**

### AI Accelerates Development, But Debugging Instincts Are Quietly Atrophying

**Coding skills are being displaced by AI, but the cognitive cost is underestimated.** A developer [shared their experience on Juejin](https://juejin.cn/post/7705961716425490447): screenshot the error, send it to AI, wait for a solution, copy and paste. The debugger panel gets opened less and less. Things move faster, but the habit of reading source code line by line and tracing call chains has quietly faded. They mention that large-scale internationalization refactors and Vue upgrades were almost entirely AI-driven. For developers who built their skills through independent debugging, today is a good moment to ask: how much time are you still setting aside for **thinking things through yourself**?

## **◉ Social Media Picks**

### WordCraft Test: English Drafts Pass, Chinese Long-Form Needs a Font Fix

**The open-source Word alternative has a Chinese display defect.** Shikong Mao [ran a full test of WordCraft](https://m.okjike.com/originalPosts/6af300c738389f5993b3a5ea) — a browser-based document tool with 17,726 Stars, 4 templates, and 11 export formats. Chinese characters are preserved intact when exporting to docx. However, characters like「署」「品」「传」appear as boxes on screen, and PDF and PNG exports show the same boxes — the issue is that the web version lacks a complete Chinese font. The verdict: works fine as a scratch pad for **English documents**, not yet usable for Chinese long-form writing. Write first, then export to docx for proofreading.
