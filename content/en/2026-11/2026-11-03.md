### DecisionRAG Uses a Hierarchical Decision Model to Replace Traditional Search Ranking

**In a corpus of 2.68 million passages, queries complete in as little as 0.7 seconds.** Gorden Sun introduced on Twitter that [the core idea behind DecisionRAG](https://x.com/Gorden_Sun/status/2117250771851022594) is to use a decision model to score all options at the same level in parallel and filter them layer by layer, rather than generating text token by token. Paper tests show a per-query cost of **$0.002–$0.023**, accuracy more than ten percentage points higher than traditional search-plus-reranking approaches, and speed roughly 30 times faster. Paper link: arxiv.org/abs/2610.35868 — recommended for engineers looking to optimize RAG retrieval speed.
