### 5. The Real Bottleneck in a Swarm of a Thousand Agents Isn't the GPU

**With 1,000 agents collaborating, the chokepoint is coordination, not compute.** Thomas Wolf, in [a summary compiled by BestBlogs.dev](https://www.bestblogs.dev/en/status/2122673947409858730), documented the process of building PyBot with DeepSeek V4.1 Flash: only **40–60 agents** were actually active at any given time, and for every **1** token written, roughly 130 tokens had to be re-read. Git merge conflicts and repeated context re-reading are the main obstacles; Brooks's Law applies perfectly here. He plans to open-source the swarm toolchain soon, so developers exploring multi-agent architectures should keep watch.
