### 1. Claude Adds Personal Finance Assistant with Read-Only Bank Linking in Beta

**Claude can now check your accounts.** The official Anthropic account announced that [Personal Finance has entered public beta in the United States](https://x.com/Gorden_Sun/status/2118717574175449437). Users can link bank accounts and credit cards via Plaid and query their financial status in natural language. The system is **read-only** — it cannot transfer or invest funds. It can summarize spending across accounts, estimate the balance remaining before payday, and flag **unusual recurring charges**.

### 2. GPT Image 2.6 Dual Models Top Image Generation Benchmark

**Image generation has a new version.** According to a summary by BestBlogs, [GPT Image 2.6 dual models have topped the image generation benchmark](https://www.bestblogs.dev/en/status/2118851088111865983?utm_source=rss&utm_medium=feed&utm_campaign=resources&entry=rss_article_item), introducing two models — Flare and Sunburst — with support for **GIF and SVG** output 🖼. Flare's median generation time has dropped from roughly 55 seconds to **30 seconds**; Sunburst takes around 2 minutes and 15 seconds but has claimed first place on the AA-Image leaderboard. API pricing is $30 per million output images, or approximately $0.21 per 1024×1024 image.

### 6. Salon Ceases Publication — 31 Years of Independent Digital Media Come to an End

**Veteran outlet Salon has officially ceased publication.** On November 5, Salon issued a farewell statement [attributing the closure to profound changes in the business environment for independent digital journalism](https://www.salon.com/2026/11/05/goodbye-from-salon/), saying its publishing model **was no longer sustainable**. Salon was founded in 1995 and ran for **31 years**, making it one of the most representative outlets of early internet news. The statement did not disclose whether the historical archive will be preserved; researchers who rely on its content should verify independently.
