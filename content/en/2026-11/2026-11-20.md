### 1. Google Releases Gemini 4 Neon, Built for Fast Completion of Complex Tasks

**New model Neon goes live.** The official Google account announced that [Gemini 4 Neon is now rolling out to paid subscribers](https://x.com/GeminiApp/status/2123174195848945768), covering Google AI Pro and Ultra users as well as equivalent Gemini App tiers. ### 4. Third-Party Evaluation of Gemini 4 Neon: Coding Costs Just Half of Competitors

### 10. AI Exploration Guide Tests Readability Improvements for Long-Form AI Translation

**The choice of prompt direction matters more than the choice of model.** The author of Telegram channel AI Exploration Guide published [a test record](https://t.me/aigc1024/27226) addressing the problem of AI translations of long passages reading unnaturally. By holding variables constant, they tested several free models with different instruction combinations, ultimately achieving usable results with **THUDM/GLM**-4-9B-0414 paired with a custom prompt. The methodology controlled for model and instruction separately, then assessed reading experience, and the finding was that prompt direction had a larger effect than model selection. Users dealing with similar translation readability issues can refer to the prompt framework the author published.
