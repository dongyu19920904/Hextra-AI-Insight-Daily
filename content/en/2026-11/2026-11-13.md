### 1. Anthropic Plans Nasdaq IPO Under Ticker ANT

**IPO filing is now public.** The [Anthropic official IPO page](https://ipo.anthropic.com/us/) shows the company plans to issue approximately **825 million** Class A shares at a price range of $70–$78 per share. Pricing is expected on November 18, with trading beginning November 19, subject to regulatory approval and market conditions. Q3 2026 revenue was $17 billion, with an adjusted operating margin of positive **15%** and a net loss of $23.3 billion, of which $21.9 billion came from non-cash valuation changes. The company will retain its public benefit corporation status, with the founders' entity controlling approximately 50.1% of general voting rights through Class F shares.

### 3. Fei-Fei Li Releases Atlas Unified World Model

**World models move toward robot training.** According to 36Kr, Fei-Fei Li gave a talk at CoRL 2026 and [released the Atlas unified world model](https://www.36kr.com/p/4066226367632263), which uses a multimodal autoregressive diffusion Transformer architecture. ### 7. The AI "Curse of Knowledge" Can Be Worked Around with Multi-Agent Collaboration

**The smarter a single agent gets, the harder it is to simulate an ignorant perspective.** In an article posted on Jike, [Pinglun Shi compiled practical thoughts on using multi-agent architectures recently](https://m.okjike.com/originalPosts/6af5589c951be4b7de9d9a8f). The core problem is that once a single agent accumulates a large body of knowledge, it struggles to think from a beginner's perspective. Multi-agent systems address this through **role division**, distributing different cognitive starting points across agents. These insights, drawn from real-world use, are a useful reference for developers designing agent workflows.
