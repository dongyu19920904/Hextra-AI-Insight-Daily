### 7. NVIDIA Snapshot Cuts Inference Cold-Start Time from Minutes to Seconds

**GPU inference workload recovery time slashed dramatically.** [BestBlogs technical summary](https://www.bestblogs.dev/en/article/ab6dbebd36) explains that NVIDIA Snapshot is an open-source Kubernetes checkpointing system that captures the initialized state of CPU and GPU memory as a portable artifact; recovery requires only reading that state rather than re-initializing. Tests with vLLM, SGLang, and TensorRT LLM show recovery times of **3.6 to 42.7 seconds**, a speedup of 8 to **36×** over cold starts 🚄. Engineers managing inference services on Kubernetes can evaluate onboarding cost using the PodSnapshot custom resource.
