### 4. ByteDance Seedream 5.5: 100 Rounds of Editing Without Drifting

**Images no longer degrade through repeated edits.** ByteDance's Seed team released **Seedream** 5.5 on November 9. According to a repost by Baoyu on X, [the official demo shows character details remaining consistent through 100 consecutive rounds of editing](https://x.com/dotey/status/2119823986603299193). The text error rate dropped from **2.8% in the previous generation to 0.4%**, and it can now output images with transparent backgrounds directly 🎨. Designers can try it today on Jimeng, Doubao, or Volcano Engine (model name: Doubao-Seedream-5.5).
