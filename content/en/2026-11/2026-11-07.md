### 1. GPT-6 Cyber Specializes in Vulnerability Discovery and Remediation

**A dedicated cybersecurity model officially launches.** The [OpenAI blog](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-cyber/) revealed two real-world deployment results: **948** working exploits developed during Linux kernel research, and **81** previously unknown high-severity vulnerabilities discovered in Firefox. ### 4. DeepSeek V4.1 Flash Inference Speedup on GB300

**Single-agent throughput rises from 596 to 848 tok/s.** According to Artificial Analysis benchmarks, [DeepSeek V4.1 Flash inference on the GB300](https://www.bestblogs.dev/en/status/2118804058672869629?utm_source=rss&utm_medium=feed&utm_campaign=resources&entry=rss_article_item) achieves roughly **1.6×** per-GPU throughput improvement. Teams running DeepSeek inference at scale should check whether they have already switched to the new vLLM environment.

### 8. LLM-Driven Neural Network Weight Modification Validated in Practice

**"Weight-space programming" runs successfully on a small model.** izx-copy noted on Jike that [the concept of LLMs modifying neural network weights has been validated in practice](https://m.okjike.com/originalPosts/6aee2c3e38389f59932b8b1e) — specifically modifying the head key projection of a roughly **0.05B** model, successfully resolving edge cases that were previously difficult to handle. The source only documents this single test; broader applicability at larger scales has yet to be verified. This opens up a new, **actionable path for localized repair** of lightweight models.

### Using a Dictionary Word List to Have AI Batch-Screen Website Ideas

**Tens of thousands of words scored in two AI rounds, narrowed down to roughly a hundred candidates.** Developer fine kewei shared on Jike that [he fed a dictionary word list with frequency data to AI, asking it to score each word from 0–9 on the question "would this word make a good website topic"](https://m.okjike.com/originalPosts/6aeda065b8cb8d00ddb81b3f), filtered for scores of 7 and above, ran a second round, and made final decisions using ahrefs keyword difficulty data. The word list is publicly available at wideseek.ai. Using this workflow he built a countdown timer tool site in **two hours**.
