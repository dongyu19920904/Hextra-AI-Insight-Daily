## Today's Top Pick

### Gemini helps you make PPTs / organize documents—account + scenario bundle, ready to list today

People have been using Gemini directly in Google Slides to make PPTs these past couple days—import templates, edit, export, all without leaving the browser. Someone said "way better value than Opus." That's a signal—a batch of people are migrating from premium subscriptions, but they don't know how to set it up or get started.

What you're selling today isn't the account itself, it's "what you can do right after getting the account."

- **What happened:** Gemini fully integrated into Google Docs ecosystem. Word/PPT/PDF full-format generation and export. Users publicly saying it beats premium subscriptions. Alternative discussion heating up
- **What to list first today:** Gemini account + "PPT/document scenario starter pack"—one account, paired with a screenshot showing how to generate and export PPTs directly in Google Slides. Buyers get it and can use it immediately
- **What to test first today:** Test two title versions: ① "No WPS, no Office—AI makes your PPT for you, account + tutorial" ② "Make PPTs without installing software, Gemini account + starter guide"—see which gets more inquiries
- **After-sales risk:** Medium. If buyers don't know how to log into the Google ecosystem after getting the account, they'll ask. Write clear login paths and precautions in the description upfront—blocks most support issues
