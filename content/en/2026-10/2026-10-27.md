### 1. Anthropic Officially Files S-1 with Nasdaq

**S-1 filed; ticker symbol ANT.** The SEC [public registration filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026067168/anthropicpbc-sx1.htm) shows underwriters are Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley; the registration statement has not yet taken effect. H1 2026 revenue was **$16.286 billion**, up **1,300%** year-over-year, with an operating loss of $693 million. The net loss of $80.7 billion is primarily attributable to fair-value changes driven by valuation increases and does not represent actual cash burn. The founders' LLC holds 50.1% voting rights; after the roadshow, the IPO is expected to price in mid-to-late November.

### 3. Microsoft Opens Voluntary Exit Program to Approximately 60,000 Employees

**About 27% of employees are eligible to opt in.** The Seattle Times reports that a [Microsoft official memo](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/10/26/introducing-a-voluntary-exit-offer/) confirms this Voluntary Exit Offer applies to employees at grade 70 and below in participating teams across Canada, India, the UK, and the US — approximately **60,000** people. The program is time-limited and voluntary, not all departments are included, and compensation amounts have **not been uniformly disclosed**. The memo does not characterize the program as a result of AI replacing workers; the actual number of participants remains to be seen.

### 4. macOS 27 Built-in Local AI Available for Free Terminal Access

**Mac ships with Apple Foundation Models — no API key required.** [Telegram channel AI Explorer's Guide](https://t.me/aigc1024/26306) reports that after upgrading to macOS 27, typing `fm chat` in the terminal invokes the model 🔒; the base version has approximately **3 billion** parameters and runs entirely **on-device**. Hard requirements are a non-China-region device and a non-China-region Apple ID; context is limited to 4,096 or 8,192 tokens, making it unsuitable for complex reasoning or long documents. Apple also provides a Python SDK, and projects like fm-proxy can connect it to agent workflows.

### 7. IBM CEO: Capability Gap for Open-Weight Models Has Narrowed Significantly

**Open-weight models can reach roughly 90% of frontier model performance at far lower cost.** The Register reports that [IBM CEO Arvind Krishna said on a quarterly earnings call](https://www.theregister.com/systems/2026/10/22/ibm-ceo-says-open-weight-models-are-now-irresistibly-good-and-cheap-on-prem/5304640) that token costs are approximately **10%–25%** of closed frontier models, and that everyday tasks such as email, initial contract review, and customer service do not necessarily require the most expensive models. He believes enterprises need only two or three server racks to build a useful on-premises AI system, though network configuration, cluster arrangement, and skilled personnel remain the primary obstacles. These are high-level executive judgments, not conclusions from rigorous controlled experiments covering all task types.
