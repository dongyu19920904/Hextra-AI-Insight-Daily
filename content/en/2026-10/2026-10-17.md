### 8. Goodfire Claims 90%+ of Final-Layer Neurons Are Interpretable

**Neural networks may be more interpretable than expected.** Goodfire's Nick Cammarata [argues in this article](https://already-clean.goodfire.com/) that polysemantic neurons are more likely branches that recursively generalize along related patterns than jumbles of unrelated concepts. With improved architectures and observation tools, roughly **99%–100%** of InceptionV1's final-layer neurons are easily interpretable; a preliminary audit of Qwen3-32B estimates that about **90%** of activation directions each have a single primary function 🔬. The author explicitly notes that these percentages depend on how thresholds are defined, and a more detailed technical paper on language models is still forthcoming.
