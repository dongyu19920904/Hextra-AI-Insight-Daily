## **🔥 Today's Top 10 Highlights**

### 1. Domestic Non-GPU Accelerators Ship More Than GPUs for the First Time

**Domestic chips have pulled ahead.** According to an [IDC report](https://www.36kr.com/p/4025420791172992), China's data center AI accelerator shipments in the first half of 2026 totaled **2.008 million units**, with non-GPU accelerators reaching 1.016 million units to surpass GPUs for the first time. Nvidia's share fell to 34%, leaving just **6 percentage points** separating it from Huawei Ascend, while Nvidia's year-over-year shipment volume declined 44%. For enterprises currently procuring compute capacity, the window for evaluating domestic alternatives is now open.

### 4. AI Content Overload May Replicate the Path of the Atari Crash

**Content supply is exploding, but attention is the real scarcity.** Jike user JimHuiHui draws a comparison in [this discussion](https://m.okjike.com/originalPosts/6ace942f756bbb66581c3f96), likening AI's impact on games and film to the 1983 Atari crash: low-quality content floods the market and erodes consumer trust.
