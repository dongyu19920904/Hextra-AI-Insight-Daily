### 10. Frontier Model Training Requires Composite Reward Schemes

**Pure human preference leads to reward hacking.** [Arena.ai research shows](https://www.bestblogs.dev/en/status/2106037792870649995?utm_source=rss&utm_medium=feed&utm_campaign=resources&entry=rss_article_item) relying solely on human preference is insufficient—models may generate visually appealing but factually wrong content or fall into "Reward Hacking"⚠️. Research adopted composite rewards, including faithfulness-based rewards evaluated via millions of human votes. This training lifted FLUX.2 by 69 points on the leaderboard, Ideogram 4 by 20 points. Offline ablation via Gemini 3.5 as judge showed that adding constraint rewards on top of preference significantly boosted win rate. Teams training generative models should consider this composite reward strategy.
