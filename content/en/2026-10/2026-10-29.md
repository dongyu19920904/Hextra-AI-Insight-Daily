### 2. DeepSeek on OpenRouter Consumes One GB300 Per Day

**Inference compute demand is lower than expected.** Opencode co-founder Dax shared on Jike that the most popular DeepSeek model on the OpenRouter platform [requires roughly one GB300 per day of requests](https://m.okjike.com/originalPosts/6ae1f69061703333c354ad1b) (valued at **$6–7 million**). The top 20 models on the platform together handle roughly **21.2T tokens** in throughput, requiring about 4 GB300s at full load 💻. Factoring in load balancing, 6–8 additional units would cover peak demand. The compute consumed by open-source model inference is far less than that of closed-source models and training workloads.

### 7. Survival LLM Integrates Offline AI with an Emergency Reference Library

**Look up first-aid and repair guides even without internet access.** Survival LLM is a free, open-source offline assistant that combines a local language model with the [Survival LLM integrated offline AI and emergency reference library](https://www.brangus.io/survival-llm) 📚. Answers cite the actual articles read and highlight relevant passages. The model and reference materials must be downloaded while online in advance; after that, all queries remain on-device 🔒. On devices with 16–31 GB of RAM, it uses roughly **6 GB** with the Granite 4.2 8B model, and the full reference library download is about **9 GB**. The author tested it with 22 questions about power outages and first aid; the larger model answered 21 correctly on the main points.

![Survival LLM integrated offline AI and emergency reference library · original image](https://www.brangus.io/survival-llm/screenshot.png "Survival LLM 整合离线 AI 与应急资料库 · 原文配图")

### 10. Personal System Iteration: Every Input Must Be Paired with an Output

**The input-output loop boosts retention.** Jike user Ling Youzhi shares the core principle of [pairing every piece of input with an output](https://m.okjike.com/originalPosts/6ae1f7d1136b2761c6eb9055) ✍️. Finish a video — write a summary. Finish a film — write a review. Finish a podcast — write notes. Finish a game — write your thoughts. Then share what you have written; it is the lowest-effort way to create leverage. This suits AI enthusiasts and programmers who want to reinforce learning through consistent output.
