### 2. arXiv Paper Submissions Hit Record, Platform Forces Rate Limiting

**Paper explosion forces hard limits.** [Starting October 1, arXiv imposed new rules](https://www.36kr.com/p/4009647948746628): each account can submit max two papers per month, no more than three papers waiting review at once, **and rejections count against your quota.** September hit **40,363 submissions**—an all-time record. Eight years back, monthly submissions were under 10,000. Staff fielded nearly 9,000 support tickets.

### 3. ECC: Performance Optimization for Agent Tooling

**Today's GitHub trending project grabbed attention.** [affaan-m/ECC](https://github.com/affaan-m/ECC) is a performance optimization system for coding agents, emphasizing skills, intuition, memory, safety, and research-first development—**works with Claude Code, Codex, Opencode, and Cursor.** Hit **897 stars** today, total count now 272,251. Built to tackle real-world agent bottlenecks in development.

### 4. Verification of Understanding Becomes New AI Prompt Best Practice

**Don't rush the task—verify the AI got it first.** Practitioners' go-to prompt is [verification of understanding as AI prompt best practice](https://m.okjike.com/originalPosts/6ac096af756bbb6658a97c94). Having the AI restate requirements often reveals you two are talking about **completely different things.** This validation step is simple but powerful, cutting AI-assisted work errors way down.

### 5. Muse Gadgets: Let AI Control DIY Hardware

**Framework for connecting custom devices to AI is here.** **Muse Gadgets** offers [SDKs for ESP32 and Linux devices](https://gadgets.muse.ai), letting developers wire stock dev boards, Raspberry Pis, displays, buttons, and sensors into AI platforms. Code ships under Apache 2.0, **each token can control up to 50 devices.** Muse reserves the right to change or terminate access—solid reference for IoT enthusiasts.

### 6. JobFlow for CodeX: Job Search Process Automation

**New automation angle arrived during fall recruiting season.** Open-source [JobFlow for CodeX](https://t.me/aigc1024/25351) hands repetitive job hunting to AI: **auto-filter positions**, generate opening messages, **track recruiter replies, maintain application progress.** Currently supports BOSS Recruitment and Hunter platforms. For developers job hunting, this saves serious time on manual sorting and follow-ups.

## **⚡ Products & Feature Updates**

### Zig 0.17.0 Released: Build System Evolution Hits Hard

**Build toolchain keeps iterating.** Zig 0.17.0 packs five months of work, 206 contributors, and 925 commits. [Build system further decouples config from execution](https://ziglang.org/download/0.17.0/release-notes.html), **introducing a Build Server Protocol for editors**, letting dev tools understand the build graph more directly. Standard library swaps DebugAllocator for SafeAllocator, adding leak and misuse checks. Before upgrading, note ZLS has temp compatibility issues; toolchain still recovering.
