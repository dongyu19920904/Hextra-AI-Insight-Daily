### 1. Google Genie 4 Brings World Models into the Age of Audio Interaction

**The world model has been upgraded.** Google DeepMind [officially released the next-generation general-purpose world model Genie 4](https://www.36kr.com/p/4044758924612485), which for the first time natively generates ambient sound and spontaneous character dialogue 🔊. A single continuous generation runs up to approximately **2 minutes**, with interactive visuals at around 24 frames per second. Three Gemini models collaborate with distinct roles: Gemini 3.8 Flash handles scene monitoring, and Gemini 4 Argon breaks down fine-grained actions — though Ultra subscribers still cannot access it directly.

### 2. Jimeng AI Launches Short-Drama Studio for End-to-End Production

**The barrier to short-drama production has dropped.** Jimeng AI has integrated script diagnosis, storyboard generation, video production, and post-production into a single platform. As reported in [Jimeng AI launches short-drama studio for end-to-end production](https://www.aibase.com/zh/news/31901), its DreamScript model supports long texts of up to **100,000 characters** and generation of up to 100 episodes. ### 3. AI Spends 24 Days Auditing the Longest Proof in Math History, Finds One Gap

**The longest proof in mathematical history has been verified by computer for the first time.** Math AI company Axiom announced on October 26 that [they used dozens of AI agents over 24 days](https://x.com/dotey/status/2115145479222665228) to complete a formal verification of the entire **Classification of Finite Simple Groups** theorem. The proof is scattered across more than 500 papers totaling approximately **15,000 pages**; one paper's proof contained a gap, which the AI patched by substituting a new proof. This is the largest-scale mathematical formalization project ever completed, and is a meaningful reference for researchers working in mathematics or logic verification.

### 4. Developer Uses 629 Articles to Measure the Telltale "Europeanized" Traits of AI Writing

**AI writing has measurable stylistic markers.** Jike user Jiangzhi Hou pushed [a developer's study using 629 articles to identify the typical Europeanized traits of AI writing](https://m.okjike.com/originalPosts/6ae0513b67c4c95640ca50c1) to GitHub. The corpus was expanded to approximately **5.2 million characters**, covering 270 pairs of articles on the same topic with the same facts. The evaluation identified key features including Europeanized balanced phrasing, dense parallel structures, and low aptness of metaphors, and used a semantic model to make approximately **430,000** judgments. Writers looking to reduce the detectability of AI-generated content can refer to this open-source evaluation framework.

### 7. A Tech Lead's Firsthand Account: AI Increased Workload Rather Than Reducing It

**AI didn't reduce work — it created more tasks.** Bold.org VP of Engineering O Ioannou [reflected on his personal blog](https://oioannou.com/blog/ai-promised-less-work/) that in August 2026 his GitHub contribution count reached **427**, surpassing his previous single-month record. His conclusion: AI **lowered the friction of execution**, but also led him to kick off more tasks simultaneously, with his attention fragmented by approval requests from multiple agents. The core frustration in this reflection is that his capacity for planning and judgment has not kept pace with the growth of his coding capacity.

### 8. Wikipedia Volunteers Identify a New AI Content Threat: Distorting Real Sources

**AI contamination has shifted from fabricating citations to misrepresenting real ones.** IEEE Spectrum interviewed a [Wikipedia volunteer about this new AI content threat: distorting real sources](https://spectrum.ieee.org/wikipedia-wikiproject-ai-writing-cleanup). She noted that the problem is escalating: the papers being cited are real, but they do not support what the article claims 🔍. She estimates that around 20% of new articles added in late 2025 were flagged as AI-generated, dropping to approximately **15%–16%** by early 2026. These figures are based on specific detection methods and do not represent the overall proportion across all of Wikipedia.

### 9. Roblox Accused of Scraping Over 145 GB of 3D Assets from Open3DLab

**A 3D asset site hit by large-scale unauthorized crawling.** Open3DLab disclosed in [a site announcement](https://open3dlab.com/news/roblox-downloaded-145-gb-of-models-from-our-sites/) that a crawler identifying itself as serving Roblox research read nearly **19,000** project pages and downloaded **145.5 GB** of files over approximately 34 hours, including 345 adult-content items and 423 items licensed for non-commercial use only. The crawler continued scraping after reading robots.txt, which the site considers a violation of its terms of service. The attribution is based on user agent and email domain; Roblox has not officially responded, and the purpose of the scraping has not been independently verified.
