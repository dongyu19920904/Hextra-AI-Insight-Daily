### 2. The Lancet: AI Extinction Risk Exceeds Nuclear Weapons

**A leading medical journal issues its highest-level warning.** 36Kr reports [The Lancet: AI Extinction Risk Exceeds Nuclear Weapons](https://www.36kr.com/p/4023950270289544). In a commission report published on October 11, The Lancet ranked **malicious use of AI** alongside nuclear conflict as one of the most likely causes of human extinction before 2100, out of 17 global threats assessed. The report was written by 35 leading experts and covers **204 countries**. It notes that the two risks could amplify each other, and further states that the potential damage from AI exceeds that of nuclear conflict.

### 4. Oneira Lets Generated Worlds Remember What You've Moved

**Pan the camera back — your changes are still there.** A team from Oxford, Monash, and other universities has proposed Oneira, which [makes objects in generated scenes interactive and persistent](https://madaoer.github.io/projects/oneira/). It uses a Coding Agent to maintain a **world-state table** that records object positions and changes, then passes that information to a video model to render the scene. Newly introduced objects can also be added to the table. The project demonstrates **targeted object interaction** and multi-step operations — developers working on world models or interactive video should check the demo.

![Oneira object interaction and state persistence example](https://madaoer.github.io/projects/oneira/static/img/teaser.webp "Oneira object interaction and state persistence example")
