## **◉ Social Media Picks**

### Claude Adds Self-Evaluation Mechanism to Proactively Flag Potential Misuse

**Claude immediately scores its own responses for safety.** Anthropic's official account posted about [the self-evaluation technique enabled since June 2026](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2115871889549865429): after each response, Claude performs a preliminary safety assessment of the entire conversation 🔒, and has already identified misuse cases that other classifiers missed. Unlike fixed classifiers, self-evaluation relies on Claude's own understanding, allowing it to handle a **broader range of potential risk types** more efficiently, since the model has already read the full conversation.
