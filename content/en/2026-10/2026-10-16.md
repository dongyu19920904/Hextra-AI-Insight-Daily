### 1. Meta Open-Sources 816B-Parameter Model That Runs Locally on Two Macs

**You can now run a massive model right on your desktop.** Meta [announced on the official blog that Muse Spark Open is now open source](https://research.meta.ai/blog/muse-spark-open), with **816B** total parameters and 42B active parameters. It natively supports text, image, video, and audio 🎬, with a context window of **1 million tokens**. Deeply optimized for Apple MLX, it can run locally on two M5 Ultra Mac Studios. Designed specifically for coding and multi-step reasoning, it suits long-horizon tasks and complex software engineering.

**You can finally see where your context is going.** The [dsh-context plugin was recommended on Juejin](https://juejin.cn/post/7696710972372009010), adding visibility capabilities 📊 to DeepSeek Harness. ## **◎ Industry Changes & Personal Impact**

### Cancer Patient Shares Experience with the U.S. Healthcare System and AI's Limitations

**AI reassurance cannot substitute for diagnosis.** JJ Maxwell [shared lessons learned after being diagnosed with cancer](https://jjmaxwell4.com/writing/things-to-know-when-you-get-cancer/), having been diagnosed with oral squamous cell carcinoma in May 2026. His wife's insistence on calling again moved his appointment three weeks earlier. He proactively requested genetic testing but was bounced between specialists, causing a delay of several months. He used AI to organize information and understand his options, but explicitly acknowledged that prior to his diagnosis the model had repeatedly reassured him it "didn't sound like cancer." A reminder to both developers and users: AI can help you understand medical information, but it cannot serve as a diagnostic tool.

## **❓ Related Questions**

### What is the claude-plugins-official project?

This is the officially maintained Claude Code plugin directory managed by Anthropic. It catalogs high-quality, reviewed plugins for extending the functionality of the Claude Code CLI. Unlike community plugin marketplaces, this repository contains only officially certified and managed plugins, ensuring quality and compatibility standards. You can find the list of available plugins and their installation instructions in the [claude-plugins-official](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-plugins-official) repository.
