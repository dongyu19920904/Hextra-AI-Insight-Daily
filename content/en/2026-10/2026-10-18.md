### 8. Passing a Single Metric Doesn't Mean an AI Agent Is Reliable Overall

**Agent evaluation requires multi-dimensional combined validation.** Arize AI's Laurie Voss used a [deliberately flawed financial analysis Agent to demonstrate](https://www.bestblogs.dev/en/video/afc9fd4fc?utm_source=rss&utm_medium=feed&utm_campaign=resources&entry=rss_article_item) a complete evaluation loop. The Agent stitched together incompatible data to produce a **116% consensus ratio** — something format checks alone would never catch. **Improvements from model upgrades** can sometimes mask degradation in other metrics. Engineers shipping Agents can refer to this approach of combining Phoenix-style tracing with CI regression.
