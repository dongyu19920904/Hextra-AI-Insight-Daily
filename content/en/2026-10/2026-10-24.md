### 10. ChatGPT Desktop App Reportedly Plagued by Heavy Disk-Write Bug

**The disk-write issue in OpenAI's desktop client has gone unfixed for several weeks.** V2EX user crysislinux [documented the latest trigger condition](https://www.v2ex.com/t/1251451#reply2): setting `pet` to `none` causes the problem to **stop immediately**, as the app continuously writes **over ten MB per second** at startup. The issue was first reported on October 17; OpenAI has not yet released a fix. Users currently on the desktop version can use this setting as a workaround.
