### 7. Figure Lets Gen-2 Robot Jump into Molten Steel for Retirement

**Retirement as tech protection.** Baoyu relays that Figure AI had its Figure 02 humanoid robot 🤖 [jump into molten steel at a Finnish foundry for retirement](https://x.com/dotey/status/2105430166876869113), with the melted metal to be made into limited commemorative items. Figure 02 once worked at **BMW factories**, ran the proprietary AI model **Helix**, and handled logistics tasks. Continued maintenance wasn't cost-effective, but disassembly would consume engineering time and delay the Gen-4 release. Foundries in the US and Mexico refused to accept robots with lithium batteries. This method prevents core actuators from leaking out while freeing up team resources.
