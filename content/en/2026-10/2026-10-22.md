### 2. ChatGPT Launches Locked Chats with Four-Digit PIN and Biometrics

**Privacy controls get an upgrade.** [OpenAI is gradually rolling out the lock feature](https://www.aibase.com/zh/news/31782), available to both paid and free users 🔒. Locked conversations are removed from regular history, pinned chats, and search results; access requires a **four-digit PIN** or face/fingerprint verification. Locked conversations and associated files are preserved in full with their own dedicated memory space. This is access control, not end-to-end encryption — it is suited for preventing privacy leaks when a device is lent to others.

### 7. Paper Proposes Classification of AI Reading Tools: Reading Companion vs. Reading Proxy, Running Shoes vs. Dumbbells

**A new framework for AI-assisted reading.** Li Jigang [recommended a paper in his post](https://m.okjike.com/originalPosts/6ad82f19bd0563695b59ca1e) proposing a four-quadrant classification 📖. The horizontal axis runs from "reader-centered" to "author-centered"; the vertical axis runs from "AI reads with me" to "AI reads for me." The paper also divides assistive tools into **running-shoe type** (vocabulary pop-ups, highlight systems, and other friction-reducers) and **dumbbell type** (recall prompts, predictive questions, and other beneficial friction-adders). This framework helps developers clarify whether they are building an effort-saving tool or a training tool.

## **◉ Social Media Picks**

### Indian Certificate Authority Uses Single-Digit Private Key, Security Community Erupts

**Key strength at its absolute weakest.** 歸藏 [retweeted a summary](https://x.com/op7418/status/2112934386475946308) in which a Jike user categorized severity levels as mild, moderate, severe, and Indian 🔐. An Indian certificate authority used trivially guessable numbers as private keys, with one key being simply **"4"** — a key that should have been over seventy digits long reduced to a single digit; others included 56, 66, 115, and 155. This configuration allows anyone to forge certificates, affecting all internet users and products that rely on certificates issued by that authority.

![Indian certificate authority weak key issue](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HVKmkQvbEAAttgZ?format=jpg&name=orig "Indian certificate authority weak key issue")

### Baoyu Explains Jev: The AI That Only Fills in Bubble Sheets

**A model that does only multiple-choice.** Baoyu [published an explainer video on how Jev works](https://x.com/dotey/status/2113046543007641942) 📝. Jev only fills in circles from a set of options predefined by the program, using fill depth to indicate confidence level — it generates no open-ended text. This makes it dozens of times faster and cheap enough that reading large volumes of text costs only a few cents; the vendor claims **"zero hallucination rate"** because it never creates content outside the given options. The video also points out where it gets answers wrong, what tasks it cannot handle, and which scenarios it is suited for, such as structured decision-making, classification, and routing.
