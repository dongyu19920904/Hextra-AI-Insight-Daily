### 7. Chrome 155 to Support JPEG XL Decoding

**Image sizes shrink another 30–50%.** The **Chrome** team announced that [starting Chrome 155, JPEG XL decoding support arrives](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/jpeg-xl-in-chrome). The format handles **lossless compression, HDR, lossless JPEG transcoding**🖼️, and fine-grained progressive decoding. Implementation uses the pure Rust decoder jxl-rs; fuzz testing and AI code reviews found no memory-safety bugs. The team suggests trying both AVIF and JPEG XL—not every image beats AVIF. This is browser decoding capability, not universal app or OS support yet.

### 8. Google Releases Cross-Modal Embedding Model

**Unified space maps text, code, images, audio, and video.** Google's blog shows that [EmbeddingGemma 2 has 740 million total parameters](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/embeddinggemma-2/), loads modules on demand, targets **on-device semantic search🔍, cross-modal retrieval, and RAG**. The model handles 8K token contexts—up to roughly **5.5 minutes of audio, 29 images, or 58 video frames**. Through Matryoshka representation learning, output vectors can truncate to 512, 256, or 128 dimensions, saving up to 6x in vector storage. Google reports that on Pixel 11 Pro quantized configs, text-only and full-modal active memory can run as low as roughly **191 MB and 567 MB** respectively.

### 10. Ted Chiang Explains Why AI Text Feels Off

**Number of choices made defines a text's soul.** My Brother Tiema relays that [Ted Chiang's 2024 New Yorker piece answers this puzzle](https://m.okjike.com/originalPosts/6ac63d22445b3350d6502205). When you write a 10,000-word story yourself, you're essentially making **10,000 choices**✍️. But when you prompt AI to generate 10,000 words, you might only make 100 to **1,000** word-level choices—AI handles the other 9,000. It's this effort of making loads of choices that gives text its soul. This also explains why the more precise and effortful your prompt, the closer the output lands to your expectations.
