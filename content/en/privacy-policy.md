### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking tech to track activity on our Service and store certain info. Tracking technologies used include beacons, tags, and scripts to collect and track info, plus to improve and analyze our Service. The tech we use may include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A cookie is a small file placed on your device. You can set your browser to refuse all Cookies or give you a heads-up when a Cookie is being sent. That said, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to refuse Cookies, our Service may use Cookies.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails may contain small electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs) that let the Company, for instance, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and get other related website stats (like recording a section's popularity and checking system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stay on your personal computer or mobile device when you go offline, while Session Cookies get deleted as soon as you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are essential for giving you access to services available through the Website and letting you use certain features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to deliver those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies identify whether users have accepted the use of Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember choices you make while using the Website, like remembering your login details or language preference. The goal of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every time you use the Website.

For more info on the Cookies we use and your choices about Cookies, check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
