### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking tech to track activity on our Service and store certain info. The tracking tech we use includes beacons, tags, and scripts to collect and track info, plus to improve and analyze our Service. Technologies we use may include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A cookie is a small file placed on your device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or flag when a Cookie is being sent. However, if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work right. Unless you've adjusted your browser settings to refuse Cookies, our Service may use them.
- **Web Beacons**: Certain sections of our Service and our emails may contain small electronic files known as web beacons (also called clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs) that let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related site stats (like recording the popularity of a section and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies get deleted as soon as you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are essential for giving you access to services available through the Website and letting you use certain features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to give you those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies figure out whether users have accepted the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember choices you make when using the Website, like remembering your login details or language preference. Their whole purpose is to give you a more personalized experience and help you avoid entering your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For more info about the Cookies we use and your choices around Cookies, check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
