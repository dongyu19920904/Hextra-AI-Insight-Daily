### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking tech to track activity on our Service and store certain info. These tracking technologies include beacons, tags, and scripts used to collect and track info, plus improve and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A cookie is a small file placed on your device. You can set your browser to reject all Cookies or flag you when a Cookie is being sent. That said, if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work right. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject Cookies, our Service might use Cookies.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might include small electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs) that let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, plus gather other related site stats (like tracking how popular a section is and checking system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies get deleted the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are must-haves for giving you access to the Service and letting you use certain features. They help verify your identity and prevent fraudulent use of your account. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be delivered, and we only use these Cookies to provide you with those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies identify whether users have accepted the use of Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember choices you make while using the Website, like your login info or language preference. The point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, so you're not stuck re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For more on the Cookies we use and your choices around them, check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
