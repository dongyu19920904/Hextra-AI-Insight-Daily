## ✨ Top 10

### 1. Meta Acquires Manus for Billions, Founder Becomes VP](https://x.com/dotey/status/2006074924868440120)

The absolute mind-blower today? Meta just announced its acquisition of Chinese AI Agent company Manus for billions of dollars. This deal is Meta's third-largest ever, right after WhatsApp and Instagram. Xiao Hong, the founder, is stepping in as a Meta VP. Manus only launched in March this year, but in just a few months, they hit a whopping $125 million ARR, and now Zuckerberg has scooped them right up. This move by a Chinese team going global with AI Agents is seriously setting a new benchmark.

---

### 2. In-depth Interview with Manus Founder: Doing a Thousand Small Things Right is More Important Than Three Big Ones](https://x.com/dotey/status/2006065780253655070)

Baoyu put together an in-depth podcast interview with Manus founder Peak, and it's packed with insights. Peak's core philosophy? Manus chose an 'intelligence-driven' rather than 'rule-driven' Agent approach. This means no pre-set workflows; the model figures out each step on its own. No MCP? It reads API docs. No API? It simulates human actions in a browser. This 'doing a thousand small things right' engineering philosophy might just be why Meta was so impressed.

![AI News Image](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/G9b6JULXkAAnNKF?format=jpg&name=orig)

---

### 3. Tencent Hunyuan Open-Sources HY-Motion 1.0: Billion-Parameter Text-to-3D Motion Model](https://x.com/shao__meng/status/2006020321787134357)

Tencent just dropped a bombshell: they've open-sourced HY-Motion 1.0, a billion-parameter text-to-3D motion model. You can describe actions in natural language and directly generate high-quality 3D character animations. Built on a Diffusion Transformer architecture, it covers over 200 action categories, and its output can plug right into existing 3D animation pipelines. Game developers and animators, you'll definitely want to check this out!

---

### 4. Study Reveals: Pro Programmers Don't Vibe, They Control](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2006063755449504154)

A recent study on 99 seasoned developers (with 3-41 years of experience) dropped some truth bombs: pro programmers use AI Agents totally differently from the 'Vibe Coding' hype you see online. Every single respondent actively controls software design and implementation, and on average, half of the AI-generated code gets tweaked. They 'tame' AI with detailed prompts, 70+ step planning documents, and project rules. The takeaway is crystal clear: AI is a collaborator, not an autopilot.
