## **Must-Read TOP 10 (In-Depth Visuals) 🔥**

*   **Making Bank 💸:** While other tech giants are busy with layoffs and cost-cutting, ByteDance just dropped a "capital shocker" on the entire industry.
    **What:** An internal email laid it all out: **"In 2025, we're going all out to recruit and retain talent."** The bonus pool is skyrocketing by 35%, and the salary adjustment budget is directly 1.5 times higher! They explicitly stated that both the upper and lower limits of compensation packages will be raised, aiming to be "not just leading, but far ahead."
    **Our Take:** This isn't just an email; it's a "charge!" signal for headhunters. AI folks, get those resumes polished!

*   **Breaking News 🇺🇸:** The Sword of Damocles hanging over TikTok's head—is it finally about to drop?
    **What:** Shou Zi Chew sent an internal letter outlining a rather clever (in a good way!) plan: a joint venture will be established to manage data security, satisfying US regulatory preferences. But here's the kicker: **e-commerce and advertising, those cash-cow businesses, will remain firmly in ByteDance's hands.** The goal is to finalize this by January 2026.
    **Our Take:** This is probably the most graceful "no-divorce" solution imaginable. As long as the core algorithms and monetization remain intact, it's still the same old TikTok.

*   **Strategic Move 📱:** Did you think ByteDance only made apps? Now they want to move directly into your phone's operating system.
    **What:** ByteDance is currently cooking up something big with **vivo, Lenovo, and Transsion**, planning to pre-install their AIGC plugins directly into phones. Simply put, this means bypassing app store restrictions and seizing the AI entry point at the system level.
    **Our Take:** This move is called "borrowing a chicken to lay eggs." For phone manufacturers, it means a new AI selling point. For ByteDance, it's the shortest path to users' fingertips.

*   **Gossip Alert 🍉:** A photo of an "Alibaba Qianwen All-Hands Meeting" went viral today, with a slogan that shockingly read "Kill Doubao"—how explosive is that?!
    **What:** Turns out, it was a huge misunderstanding. Alibaba insiders responded, saying it was "obviously fake." Later, someone dug up the truth: **the image was actually generated by a competitor's (reportedly Doubao's) AI painting tool.**
    **Our Take:** LOL, using a rival's AI to generate a slogan attacking them, only to be exposed. This isn't just a business battle; it's a public lesson in AI-generated image identification.

*   **Controversy 🍎:** When buying an iPhone, due to differing app store commissions, we might have been acting as the "richest suckers globally."
    **What:** Under legal pressure, Apple caved in Japan, opening up to third-party stores and payments. However, among the top four global markets, **only China remains a solid block of dual monopoly**, with the highest commission rates worldwide.
    **Our Take:** Tim Cook might say he loves the Chinese market, but his actions honestly show he's collecting the "highest tax rate." Consumer complaints alone won't fix this; we'll have to see if a mandatory regulatory hammer comes down later.

*   **Shake-up 🛍️:** Pinduoduo's "chop a deal" tactic is about to get an upgrade.
    **What:** Zhao Jiazhen has been appointed Co-Chairman. The signal behind this is clear: **they're going all-in on the supply chain.** They plan to invest heavily over the next three years to overhaul their supply chain, aiming to recreate another Pinduoduo (Temu).
    **Our Take:** Don't just focus on the domestic "10 Billion Subsidy" anymore; the current script is "Chinese factories + Pinduoduo algorithms" to compete in the global market.

*   **Pitfall Avoidance ⚠️:** A fellow netizen wanted to set up an an AI story generation project (Narratium), but their server instantly turned into someone else's mining rig.
    **What:** Right after deployment, a mining Trojan latched on like a stubborn plaster, impossible to remove, keeping the CPU at 100% load long-term. This usually happens from using unclean Docker images or scripts with hidden payloads.
    **Our Take:** When playing with open-source AI projects, **never run naked in this era full of traps.** Check your scripts, use non-root privileges, and keep your firewall tight, or you'll just be paying the hackers' electricity bill.

*   **Rant 🤬:** Even Luo Yonghao couldn't take it anymore, posting on WeChat Moments to complain about Shanghai Telecom.
    **What:** He subscribed to a dedicated gigabit broadband, but the actual speed wasn't even a tenth of that. After customer service fixed it, it broke again within days. Luo Yonghao stated that if it's not resolved, he'll "go crazy online."
    **Our Take:** If even a celebrity has to threaten to "go crazy" to protect their rights, ordinary people facing such "falsely advertised" broadband probably just have to grit their teeth and restart their routers.

*   **Glitch 🐛:** Google's AI models are pretty powerful, but their user system seems like a joke.
    **What:** A netizen tried to snag some Google One perks (or just use it normally), but got stuck in an infinite loop on the "verify eligibility" screen. Click verify -> no response -> click again -> still no response.
    **Our Take:** This is a classic case of "big tech arrogance." Algorithms can calculate everything under the sun, yet they can't fix a simple webpage navigation bug.

*   **Discussion 🧠:** There are so many AI models these days, it's dizzying—which one should you use?
    **What:** The community is debating the difference between Claude/GPT's Thinking mode (long thought) and the Pro version.
    **Our Take:** **Pro Tip:** If you're architecting code or performing complex logical deductions, you absolutely *must* enable "long thought" mode; it can correct many logical flaws. If you're just polishing emails or doing simple Q&A, the Pro version is sufficient, don't waste those expensive "thinking credits."
